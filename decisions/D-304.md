# D-304: Trusted-core growth for #4170 (MIG-4-recovery-r4c/r4e)

Date 2026-10-31. Source: D-201 (Mark, option C on the Decisions 8 card cmsg_01R91wiieBzMSvPKFsAJVvuK2G1EAorZBbTe5NhkuhSdtz, 2026-10-27), whose route B is approved by a hardware key's tap, and SPEC REC-4, which makes a recovery-key rotation a tier-4 action. Standing rule: D-159.

**Decision.** PR #4170 (issues #4128 and #4160) may grow the trusted core by +96 lines (`tools/trusted_base.py measure`, 40336 to 40432 against main at a756821), in `kernel` and `kernel/proc`. After a route-B restart the kernel names the card it holds by the card's backup recipient ID (`recoverycardid`, no bits) and answers `recoverycard` only on an anchor's signature over `kernel.RecoveryCardHash(id)`; a phone passkey's signature, or one over another card, is `ErrRotateSig`. The encoded answer clears its bits. Merged r4b (#4108) answered the bits to any owner-page session, including one signed in with the phone passkey alone (#4108 Security point 2).

**Review.** The reviewer checks each added core line against D-201 and REC-4: the change only narrows who reads the card route B makes; growth beyond that is a finding, not a waiver.
