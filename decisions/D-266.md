# D-266: A paired device's mDNS names come from the enforcer

Date 2026-11-01. Auto-decided under the no-lens-gets-worse rule, from the builder of #4401 (relayed by the coordinator).

**Decision.** A paired device's mDNS names come from the enforcer. Its relay also listens hear-only on every Devices interface, with nothing forwarded and the same read limits and parse caps as today. When a link pairs a device that pins no names, the enforcer's page fills in the names it heard that device prove as its own, on the device's own address. The pairing card lists each name before the passkey step. The untrusted core can never introduce a name.

**Rejected.** The enforcer reports names to the core, which composes them. It keeps the same parse surface, adds hops, and lets a compromised core pin names.
