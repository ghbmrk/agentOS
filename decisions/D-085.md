# D-085 (2026-10-09)

Convergence rules (OPERATING §2, §4, §5, §6; packages CONV-0 to CONV-6).

**Why.** Merging is fast (421 PRs merged 2026-10-02 to 2026-10-09, about 100 on 2026-10-09), but open work grew faster than it closed. On 2026-10-09: 238 open BOARD rows, 183 of them absent from LATER.md's release/later audit; 83 review follow-up rows, 80 not merged; 186 open PRs, 136 of them drafts and 103 untouched since before 2026-10-08; no LEDGER reading after 2026-10-04. Each merged tier-A PR raised 3 to 7 follow-up rows, and follow-ups raised their own (SR3-4 → SR3-4-f1..f5; DEP-3 → DEP-3-r1..r7 → DEP-6-r1 → DEP-8-r1). Two clauses drove it: "when unsure, pick the stricter class" and "tier-A security findings are never later", applied to assurance tooling's messages as well as to the product.

**What changes.**

1. A release finding names its acceptance test or invariant and one failure path.
2. Unsure between release and later: `later`, tagged `recheck`.
3. On assurance tooling, only a possible false pass is release; messages, diagnostics and remedy text are later.
4. A finding on a follow-up of a follow-up is later unless it is a blocker on the cited IDs, a false pass or an exploit path.
5. One PR's review findings make one follow-up brief.
6. No UX lens pass on tier-A diffs confined to assurance tooling; Potency owns messages that could read as a pass.
7. A 48-hour idle limit on PRs and `building` rows; CODEX-1 drafts exempt.
8. Questions for Mark in docs/MARK-QUEUE.md.
9. Daily METRICS with convergence measures; a daily LEDGER reading.
10. A pre-release sweep of every `recheck` line and every tier-A `later` line.

Tier-A security findings on broker paths keep their rule unchanged, and no review stage is removed for product code.

**What it costs.** Items 2 to 4 defer some hardening until after the first release. Nothing is dropped: each deferral is a LATER.md line, and item 10 re-reads them before ship.

**Guard (revert rule).** CONV-0 measures promotions from later to release and `Defect:` lines per week. If either runs above its baseline (the two weeks before this decision) two weeks in a row, items 2 to 4 are reverted by a new decision row.
