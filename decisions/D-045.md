# D-045 (2026-10-05)

HOST-1b clock conditions (HW-8, TIM-1). Security: **T1** mask timesyncd, refuse timedated SetTime and SetLocalRTC, or mask timedated; chrony has no rtcsync, rtcfile or hwclockfile. **T2** VM test: STA_UNSYNC still set after sync and after more than 11 minutes, RTC unchanged; image test: no hwclock binary or unit. **T3** the floor never steps the clock and only feeds Earliest and Latest (expiry checks use Latest, waits use Earliest); it only advances, only from Verified time, except that an NTS sync replaces a floor ahead of it (a corrupt or forged file); persisted atomically; floor rollback is carried with the TPM NV anchor. **T4** NTS sources first (time.cloudflare.com, nts.netnod.se, ptbtime1.ptb.de), Debian pool as fallback; Verified means an NTS sync, or plain NTP with minsources 3 agreeing; NITZ counts only as a tie-break within a bound of the floor. Amended on #177 (L3 MUST 1): plain NTP and carrier time verify only at or above floor − tolerance, and only NTS can lower a floor that is ahead of the clock. **T5** a chronyc error, "Not synchronised", or stratum 0 or 16 is unsynced. **T6** /dev/rtc stays root 0600; DevicePolicy=closed on root units; Now and TOTP stay on the box clock (K7). **T7** (accepts Potency C1) the RTC−UTC offset is learned only at a Verified sync, rounded to 15 minutes, within ±14 h, persisted atomically; a boot one-shot does settimeofday to RTC − offset before chronyd starts, never writes the RTC, leaves the clock unverified under the floor, and does nothing when no offset is known. Amended on #177: learned only at an NTS sync and keyed to the host PC. UX-H1b-1: no separate line; "Time check: not confirmed since start at HH:MM (waiting for network or phone-network time). Nothing to do.", or the Disagree wording if anything is restricted. Potency C2: not counting wrong codes while the clock is unverified was rejected; wrong codes still count, and the refusal and STATUS say "My clock isn't set yet, so codes may not work. Open my Wi-Fi page to set it." Potency R1 (phone-attested time on the Wi-Fi page) accepted for part 2 under **T8** attested time strictly later than the last persisted one, the code's step recorded as used across reboots, wrong attempts count toward the lockout and per-phone limit; **T9** attested time is for codes and waits only, never update, TUF, certificate or 180-day checks, never advances the floor; **T10** at or after the floor and within ±24 h of RTC − offset when an offset is known, else refused with fixed wording
