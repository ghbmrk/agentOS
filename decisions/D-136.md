# D-136: Vehicles and health trackers as devices

Date 2026-10-19. Source: Mark, Questions thread, 2026-10-19 15:47:11Z (cmsg_01R63S2yrPkq32thy8WQCuvDVGaXiXmyYxRXpvGk4yEUGg): "Vehicles aren’t out of scope as devices".

**Decision.** SPEC §14 no longer lists vehicles as out of scope; medical devices stay out. Vehicles are §10B devices.

**Shape (proposed by the Questions thread; a reading for Mark's typed line).**
1. Reached through the vendor cloud as an adapter, route order per ADP-3 (API such as a fleet API, then web, then app). Vehicles are cellular, so the enforcer cannot reach them directly (NET-1); the gate is the core's owner-signed destination list.
2. Reads (location, charge, status) are `read`.
3. Unlock, remote start, climate, horn and lights, trunk and movement are `actuate-physical`, high-risk under CH-10 for the unsafe directions.
4. Vehicles join the DEV-10 safety or access classes; unlock, start and movement are never pre-allowed.

No new mechanism, owner reading, ask or tap.

**Also (Mark, 15:48:41Z, cmsg_01R63S2yrPkq32thy8WQCuvDLuqebX5MBdaxT5kd6uBQsa: "Fitness devices, Health trackers etc also not out of scope").** Fitness and health trackers (Oura, Whoop, Garmin, Fitbit, Dexcom) are in scope as devices and accounts via vendor cloud API or website; reads are `read`, data private under REV-6. Phone-only sync stays unreachable until an app-phone route exists (NET-1 unchanged). Medical devices (Mark, 15:51Z card cmsg_01R63S2yrPkq32thy8WQCuvDHYBHxvnsbqGnkBFuexecGc: "Reads only"): data reads (CGM, blood-pressure cuff, scale) are in scope; controlling one (pumps, dosing) stays out of scope.
