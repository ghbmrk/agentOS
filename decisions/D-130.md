# D-130: Mark's tap only for gate files and interpretive spec diffs

Date 2026-10-17. Source: Mark, Decisions 6 thread 2026-10-17 20:06:48Z ("Why am I merging these things I already approved - unnecessary bottle necking on me", cmsg_01R91wiieBzMSvPKFsAJVvuKRMNcN2XShk67LfMAS95R7T), then "Gate files only" on the D-125 card with his condition at 20:15:43Z ("Gate files only as long as spec dif isn’t open to interpretation + I’ve already approved", cmsg_01R91wiieBzMSvPKFsAJVvuKEW8WD489cRR55gAKtS4GS4). Narrows D-125 and CLAUDE.md's SPEC.md rule. Procedure: docs/OPERATING.md §4, §7.

**Decision.**
1. **Gate files keep the per-head tap.** A PR for which `tools/premerge.py --needs-mark` prints `mark` (agent rules, CI and merge gates, risk tiers, freeze lists: the D-125 list, unchanged) merges only on Mark's own approval of that PR at its head, exactly as D-125 says.
2. **A spec diff that transcribes his decision needs no tap.** A PR changing SPEC.md merges on its reviews' accepts, like any other PR, when both hold: it transcribes a decision Mark already made himself (a card tap or his typed words, cited by message id), and a fresh reviewer confirms the diff adds no interpretation beyond that decision. The reviewer fetches each cited message, checks that a human (Mark) wrote it, and states it on its own line of the accept: `Transcribes: <message id>[, <message id>]`. Every accept on that PR, delta reviews included, repeats the line. A judgement call the decision does not settle (a default, a number, a scope, a wording that changes meaning) is not a transcription: the reviewer names it, and it goes to Mark on a Decisions card; the PR then merges on his approval at its head (D-125's procedure) or after a push that removes the point.
3. **A tap carries across main-merges.** Mark's approval of a head carries to a later head when `python3 tools/premerge.py <approved-sha> HEAD` prints `same` (main merged in, the PR's own diff unchanged), as D-125 already said. Any other push needs a delta review and, for 1 or an interpretive 2, a new approval.

**Enforced.** `premerge.py --check` (the required `premerge` status) prints `needs-mark SPEC.md` for a SPEC.md PR unless the newest accept carries the `Transcribes:` line or a trusted `Mark-approved: <message id> · head <SHA>` comment names a head whose diff equals the current one. Decision records (DECISIONS.md, `decisions/`) are tier C and stay ungated; a record that attributes a decision to Mark is reviewed against his cited message the same way, and an interpretive point goes to a card.

**Not changed.** The `--needs-mark` list, the review tiers (SPEC.md stays tier B), arm-at-accept for every other PR, and the D-125 procedure for the tap itself.

**Reasons.** The tap protects Mark's decisions, not his clicks. On gate files any diff can loosen how Claude is permitted or reviewed, so a person checks the exact head. A spec diff that only writes down what he already chose adds nothing for him to decide, so tapping it again is the bottleneck he named. The `Transcribes:` line makes the reviewer's claim explicit and checkable by the gate; it cannot prove the claim, so the fresh review is the control and an interpretive point always reaches him.
