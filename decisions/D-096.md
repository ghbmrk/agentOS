# D-096: Scope freeze and PR cap

Date 2026-10-10. Source: Mark, simplification thread ("Yes urgently pursue all simplifications", then "Approval to all", 2026-10-10), on the architecture simplification review (findings 4 and 5).

**Freeze.** Until acceptance gate G2 (A4, A5, A9) passes, no new rows, follow-ups or builds for: the open-source bridge (cleanroom, hint, pubid, pubsend), the second line (sipsign, smsapi, sendrules, secondline, sipline), fuzz in the loop (loop7, machprobe, sockprobe; #710 is already a frozen draft) and learning sources beyond one candidate source. The 30 queued BOARD rows in the first and third groups read `queued (frozen until G2, LATER FRZ-x, D-096)`; the groups are LATER rows FRZ-oss, FRZ-line2, FRZ-fuzz and FRZ-learn. Rows already in review or building finish or are handed off under the idle limit; the coordinator decides which. `childproc` is trusted core (SIM-core) and its rows are not frozen. SIM-* rows and the W5-D drafts are untouched (D-095). A frozen row is revived by naming the acceptance test it serves. Every other queued row is re-filed against a G1 or G2 test or moves to LATER (FRZ-refile).

**Cap.** At most about 10 open PRs per lane (docs/LANES.md; the primary team is one lane), drafts included. The CODEX-1 exemption from the 48-hour idle limit ends: held drafts count against the cap and are finished, handed off or closed with their branch kept like any other PR. This supersedes the exemption clause of D-086 item 7. Exception: the W5-D drafts that D-095 keeps open as reference are exempt from the cap and the 48-hour rule until SIM-cases lists their cases, and follow both after that. Closing is the coordinator's act; this decision closes no PR. WIP-1 brings `tools/metrics.py` in line.

**Reasons.** No gate is recorded as passed, yet work proceeds on all five. The frozen groups serve G4, G5 or the later criterion, and the draft exemption is how the W5 stack grew to dozens of open drafts. Frozen code rots and some will need rework when it thaws; if nothing frozen is ever revived, the cut was too small.
