# D-146: Limit the clerk; hardware keys are the root

Date 2026-10-21. Sources: Mark, tap "Limit the clerk" on the Decisions card cmsg_01R63S2yrPkq32thy8WQCuvDEyNeBfLkrFtSzV7MZ6RNDJ, 2026-10-21 13:17:38Z; and "Okay plan on hardware key + link ideal key to buy", Decisions thread 2026-10-21 13:32:42Z (cmsg_01R63S2yrPkq32thy8WQCuvD9jYLoemEJqwywXVvEAEws3), approving the plan at cmsg_01R63S2yrPkq32thy8WQCuvD7qSnWVMryFYptuVBdwhpQH and the UX at cmsg_01R63S2yrPkq32thy8WQCuvDYQ8AFK3YmRWMdjzRKn5jZc.

**Decision.**
Limit the clerk; hardware keys are the root. (1) The root's holder is its passkey: only a passkey tap uses a bare root (a one-off over the clerk's limits) or derives from it. (2) Every key off the root is a clerk key with an expiry and a daily spend cap (`key.ClerkKey`, life 30 days, renewed by any passkey tap); the chain check refuses a first link without both. (3) The trust anchors are the owner's two device-bound hardware keys; a phone passkey (which may be cloud-synced) is enrolled under them by an anchor's signature, at once, and a revoke cuts one off for good (lost phone: the hardware key enrolls the new phone and cuts off the old). This supersedes the clause "root = phone passkey" of the storage plan Mark approved 2026-10-20 02:55Z (cmsg_01R63S2yrPkq32thy8WQCuvDD13zebQRJRyCroStBypjxn). (4) Roots and first links are grants in the log (KEY-1); a grant is live after a hold of about 24 hours with an owner notice, and a revoke cancels it; a renewal at the same limits is live at once. Setup grants go as one batch: one hold, one key tap. (5) A big widening (more than the owner-settable line, default double a limit, or a new kind of power) also needs a second enrolled passkey's signature, one of them a hardware key; so does a very large one-off. Ordinary steps within the clerk's limits never wait. Narrows D-145

**Why.** A compromised core must not hold full power, and a passkey prompt does not show what it signs: the hold and its notice give the owner a window against a tricked tap, and the second passkey guards the large changes. A synced phone passkey would make the cloud account the real root, so device-bound hardware keys anchor it.

**Where it lives.** Items 1 to 4 are built in KRN-passkey (`broker/kernel/key`, `broker/kernel`, `broker/clerk`, see broker/kernel/ASSUMPTIONS.md KRN-root-limit, KRN-grant, KRN-passkeys); item 5 is the next PR (KRN-cosign).
