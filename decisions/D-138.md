# D-138: Guest cloud-off

Date 2026-10-19. Source: Mark's tap "Cloud-off" at 17:50:49Z on the Decisions card cmsg_01R63S2yrPkq32thy8WQCuvDJdKSrm4BFLyLR3QbAuU5aR, after he asked what it meant (17:49:47Z) and read the plain explanation. The proposal came from item 5e of the combined review of PR #2416 (issue #2417), not from Mark's words; his tap adopts it.

**Decision.** A CLI guest's declaration switches off the provider's cloud and remote features (cloud or hosted tasks, remote execution, session sync or history, telemetry, update checks, notifications, and provider-hosted tools such as web search and fetch), as CRED-5 W4 does for a worker-held provider agent. Normal model calls keep working. A feature with no known off switch is `[Open]`, and its endpoints are refused at egress. Written into ARC-3 and ARC-6(a). Refines D-131.

**Why.** A broker-held CLI guest holds a placeholder and the broker injects the credential into declared endpoints. A provider feature that moves work or data off the box would use the credential for more than model calls, outside ARC-6.

**Not here.** The per-CLI switch lists, the `Validate` check and the egress tests belong to brief ARC3-guest-cloud-off (#2417), which builds after this merges.
