**Decision.** The lost-card route (D-201 route B) replaces the store's passphrase slot in the same one write as the recovery slot and the backup recipient, and the new card prints both. This supersedes D-201 route B's clause "its passphrase still opens the store": after a lost-card rotation the old passphrase opens nothing.

**Trusted-core growth.** PR #4226 (issue #4130) may grow the trusted core by +130 lines (`tools/trusted_base.py measure`, 40660 to 40790 against main at 77b565e), in `card/words` (a new known package: the EFF wordlist, with the passphrase derived from the card's bits by HKDF), `clerk/seal` (`ReplaceAndPut` takes several slot swaps) and `cmd/agentos-unlock`.

**Review.** The reviewer checks each added core line against D-201 and Mark's rule above; growth beyond the passphrase-slot replacement is a finding, not a waiver.
