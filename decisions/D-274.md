**Decision.** PR #4671 (the #4574 and #4649 kernel trip package) may grow the trusted core by +78 lines (`tools/trusted_base.py`, 41165 to 41243, as measured by the builder), in `broker/kernel` and `broker/decoy`, with no new linked packages. A trip stops only a session its key verified for.

**Review.** The reviewer re-measures against the base at merge (the baseline moves as other core rows land, for example D-273) and checks each added core line against the two issues; growth beyond them is a finding, not a waiver.
