# D-125: Mark's approval merges Mark-only PRs

Date 2026-10-16. Source: Mark, Decisions thread 2026-10-16 15:59:47Z ("I shouldn't be a bottleneck", cmsg_01R91wiieBzMSvPKFsAJVvuK76nhVYJJwchmXMCP6GyLUs) and 16:01:12Z ("Agents merging all as long as comes from my approval is fine - deciding on own without me in the loop is not fine.", cmsg_01R91wiieBzMSvPKFsAJVvuK6DT88U1bKuBRitJ6xebzDR). Amends D-120 item (4). Procedure: docs/OPERATING.md §7.

**Decision.** A PR for which `tools/premerge.py --needs-mark` prints `mark` no longer waits for Mark to press Merge himself. Any session may merge it once all of these hold:
1. Mark approved that PR himself: his tap on a Decisions card, or his own typed message, naming the PR number and the head SHA being merged (short form is enough; the card shows both). The merging session fetches the cited message itself and confirms a human author (Mark), this PR, this head and, for a card, the merge option tapped. A pending card or its recommended option, a standing rule (including "if all reviews accept just merge"), a coordinator note, a review accept or an agent's paraphrase is not his approval.
2. Every review the tier requires accepted that head, and CI is green on it.
3. The merging session first comments `Mark-approved: <message id> · head <SHA>` on the PR, then merges with the head pinned (REST `sha`, or MCP `expectedHeadSha`). It never arms auto-merge on such a PR.

The approval carries forward across a new head only when `python3 tools/premerge.py <approved-sha> HEAD` prints `same` (main merged in, no PR line changed). Any other push needs a delta review and a new approval.

**Not changed.** The `--needs-mark` path list, the review tiers, and the arm-at-accept route for every other PR (D-120 items 1-3).

**Reasons.** Mark pressing Merge made him the bottleneck on mechanical work he had already decided. What the guard protects is his decision, not his click: a PR on these paths can loosen how Claude is permitted or reviewed, so the agent may carry out his approval but never substitute its own. Pinning the head keeps the approval bound to the diff he approved.
