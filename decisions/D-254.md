# D-254 (2026-10-31)

The alert light is saved for when an attack is known. It extends D-233 (breach decoys: the alert is a light only, controlled by the enforcer).

**Rule (Mark):** "The light should be saved for when an attack is known."

**What the light shows:** only events that mean an attack is known, such as a decoy used or a reader release outside its window, count or cap.

**Open point:** a byte-cap overrun lights the bulb only if the open cut-or-block card (#4111) makes it a trip. This row does not decide that; Mark's tap on that card does, and gets its own row.

**Where everything else goes:** the enforcer's page, plus its text or call (as relayed by the coordinator).

No new mechanism (D-151); no bound is loosened.
