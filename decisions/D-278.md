# D-278: Trusted-core growth for #4681 (DECOY-match-wrap-4)

Date 2026-11-02. Source: issue #4681 (KRN-12), items 1 to 3. Standing rule: D-159.

**Decision.** The build PR for issue #4681 (DECOY-match-wrap-4) may grow the trusted core by +52 lines (`tools/trusted_base.py`, 41256 to 41308 against main 82bf72ca, as measured by the builder), all in `broker/decoy/match/match.go`, with no new linked packages. The lines do two things:
1. They index each `\` by its neighbouring bytes, which brings an attacker-written `x\n` stream back to plain-text speed (about 9 times faster than before, as the builder measured). This keeps the matcher's cost bounded under hostile input.
2. After a break, they match the rest of a form with the later held breaks read back, which closes items 2 and 3 of the issue.

It follows D-267, D-268, D-270 and D-273.
