# D-307: The ultimate form

Date 2026-11-02. Source: Mark 2026-11-02 00:33:30Z, "Do it." (cmsg_01R91wiieBzMSvPKFsAJVvuK8WN5pNrRaT2qYxKwyn9d7f), answering the decision "Make this form §0 of the spec, and hold the owner-layer rebuild to it" in thread cmsg_01R91wiieBzMSvPKFsAJVvuKHf1Gkq4Npgr2ofDhoScfgm. The form answered Mark's ask of 2026-11-01 21:30Z: "How to make the design more beautiful. Like a math proof, the best designs are typically extremely novel and innovative but also very simple. Before implementing this new spec, want the ultimate form to be expressed".

**Decision.** SPEC §0 states agentOS as three objects and one function. What happened is the log, what exists is declared, and what may happen is keyed; the kernel lets an effect out only when a key covers it. Keys descend from the two hardware keys and only narrow. Only the owner widens authority, with a proof that costs more to fake than the key's reach is worth (KEY-9) and never less than CH-3 asks. D-151's eight mechanisms fall to three: the log, declarations and keys. The graph, narrowing, the witness, recovery and the planner become properties of these three, or sources of events. The owner-layer rebuild (the rung as a caveat, surfaces as declarations) is held to this form.

**The goal, read as mathematics.** Mark's goal (HW-14) is "massive leverage at low effort thru combinations of hugely private data and credentials that's only possible through extreme structural security". That is an objective under a constraint: maximize what gets done per second of the owner's attention, while bounding what any one failure can lose. Four axioms, each already in the spec:
1. Authority comes only from the owner, proven physically (OWN-1, OWN-10, KEY-4).
2. Anything learned may be wrong or hostile (KRN-3, D-153).
3. Any one failure domain may fall (CLK-10 counts the owner's safeguards this way).
4. The owner's attention is the scarce resource (OWN-7, OWN-8).

**The lemma.** Value and danger are the same quantity. The leverage comes from combinations of accounts, and a failure exposes the same combinations: a mailbox that receives the card's reset link reaches the card. Limiting reach to be safe gives up the goal, and ignoring it gives up the safety, so authority is priced by its reach (KEY-9).

**Three laws.**
1. Models propose; only keys act.
2. Anything narrows at once; only the owner widens, at the price the reach sets.
3. A reversible effect runs and shows `UNDO`. An irreversible one waits a hold that grows with its novelty and its key's worth, unless the owner approves it; the hold may be zero for a step the owner has accepted before (KEY-8).

**What follows with no further rules.**
- Injection is bounded by keys and labels.
- STOP always works, since stopping is narrowing.
- Home grants and away spends, since the surest proofs live at home.
- Every surface is a declaration.
- Recovery starts again from the hardware keys (REC-2).
- A change to the box is an effect on it (§8).
- Repeated approvals become keys (KEY-5).
- The three boxes follow from axioms 2 and 3. No component holds untrusted input, private data and a way out at once (the agents' rule of two, Meta, 2025-10-31). Because the core itself may fall, the internet sits behind the edge and the home behind the enforcer.

**Borrowed and new.**
- *Borrowed:* keys that only narrow (macaroons, NDSS 2014, which KEY-1 follows); planning over handles with a quarantined reader (CaMeL, arXiv:2503.18813); the agents' rule of two; and the trusted path.
- *New:* reach as the price of authority; presence as a caveat (with the owner-layer rebuild); one failure-domain test for the owner's accounts and for the box itself; and one window sized by worth as well as novelty.

**Where it could be wrong.**
- Reach is only as good as the graph. Unknown edges count as reached, which over-prices some keys until the graph learns them.
- Prices move when the graph grows, so the kernel narrows first (KEY-9).
- A rule is harder to audit than a table, so CH-3's rows stay as its floor and its test cases.
- The form makes the box safe, not good: planner quality still decides usefulness (HW-16).

**Scope.** This decision restates §0 and adds KEY-9, and the SPEC diff shrinks the spec (D-152). Later rows, each held to this form:
- the fixed worth table;
- the reach computation in the key-check library;
- the owner-layer rebuild that adds the rung caveat.

The rearchitecture proposal's own decisions still need their own answers: deleting the code generator (which waits on QUAL-page-ca), the grid and the inline unlock.
