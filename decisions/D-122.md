# D-122: an app login on a box-owned dedicated phone counts as broker custody

Date 2026-10-15. Source: Mark, project chat 2026-10-15, owner approvals at 14:03Z, 17:57Z, 18:48Z and 18:51Z. Work item #1843 (APP-ROUTE-SPIKE, route 4: a dedicated stock Android phone with the box as device owner).

**Decision.** A login held inside an app on a box-owned dedicated phone counts as broker custody of that credential only if all four hold:
1. the phone has no SIM;
2. the phone is not on the owner's number;
3. every commit tap is gated by the approval tiers (CH-10);
4. the phone is never the approval phone: it never holds the code generator the owner's approval codes come from (CH-4).

If any one fails, the login is not broker-held and the route does not qualify.

**Reasons.** The phone is then a broker-controlled device that cannot receive the owner's texts or calls, cannot take an effect without the owner's approval at the usual tier, and cannot approve its own actions.

**Not changed.** #1843 stays a spike under the G1 freeze (D-118); this settles the custody question for route 4 and builds nothing.
