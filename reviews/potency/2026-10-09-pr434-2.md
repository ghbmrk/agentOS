Verdict: accept

Tier A, Potency lens only. Reviewed from `git diff origin/main...pr434`, UPD-1, UPD-1a, OP-4, OP-5 and the L3 and Security comments. Question: can update settlement still block updates, security fixes included?

- The main potency loss on main is closed: a transient I/O error or cut no longer leaves `Applying` set for good, which blocked every later update. One power cut no longer blacklists a valid release; the cost is one extra try, bounded and recorded (A5).
- Owner UNDO of a staged image is refused until it starts. On main that UNDO wedged the applier, so in practice no capability is lost; the owner can still undo after the restart.
- The remaining wedge is Security S1 (commit and confirm durable, applier save cut, next boot on the old root: `StageFailed` fails forever and `Tick` refuses every update). Rare, but its cost is the same as F4's: security fixes stop. Agreed `release`, before W5b; no change of class.

Points:
1. **release (UPD-1; same fix as UX point 1 here and on #430)**: an orphaned staged adoption (not handed and never rescheduled) costs no update capacity, but it blocks the owner's UNDO and Recheck's revert of that adoption for good. Settling it when its release is dropped or superseded removes the cost; no separate row.
