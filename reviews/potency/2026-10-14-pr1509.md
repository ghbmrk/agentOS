## Potency
- **#1474 is done.** `compare_manifests.py` fails on a missing, extra, empty, unparsable or package-less manifest. `|| ok=0` makes the job fail. The normal run prints the same non-zero count for both sides.
- **#1475 is done.**
  - The CI listing showed Bluetooth was present in the kernel-modules initrd, so the PR adds `KernelModulesInitrdExclude=`.
  - `finish_image.py` fails the build on any Bluetooth match in any initrd the entry boots.
  - I20 records the result, and the hardware step is on #1113.
- **G1 on the M6 Ultra loses nothing.**
  - The excludes affect only the kernel-modules initrd, never `/usr`.
  - `mt7921e`, NVMe, xHCI and dm-verity stay in, and the test pins that.
  - No Ethernet driver matches `bluetooth`, `/bt(usb|mtk)\.ko` or `/wireless/rsi/`.
  - Boot is proven by CI's boot test.
  - Dropping Redpine `rsi_91x` costs nothing, because the initrd needs no Wi-Fi.
- **It is maintainable.**
  - The exclude regexes are short and commented, and the build check fails closed.
  - If a future kept module pulls Bluetooth back in, as `rsi_91x` did, the build fails rather than shipping it.
  - The 30-line cpio reader is justified: a truncated archive raises an error, and `cpio -t` would not.
