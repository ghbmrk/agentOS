Verdict: accept

Tier A, Potency lens only. Reviewed from the diff against its merge-base with `origin/main`, OSS-2, A12 and cleanroom C14. Question: does durable completion cost clean-room output or throughput?

- It gains output. Before this, a lost or truncated artifact behind a completed manifest stuck its hint for good, because matching hints coalesced into a dead artifact. Now it is quarantined and rebuilt under the same job and artifact ID, so the work comes back with no owner action and no duplicate artifact.
- The costs are bounded and recorded: file and directory syncs per artifact; one hash read of every artifact at open (`CR-open-cost`, LATER); up to `maxRepairs` rebuilds before a job is logged failed, which stops storage that keeps losing data from burning clean-room time without end. A repaired job may exceed `MaxQueue` by one entry, which takes no hint's place.
- A failure to requeue or quarantine at open makes `New` fail, so the builder does not start. That fails closed, needs a queue or store that cannot be written, and the same storage would fail the build anyway.

Points: none.
