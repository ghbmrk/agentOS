This is the tier A lens screen, Potency pass. Security is reviewed in its own session.

- **Gain (OP-4, G1).**
  - Before: `Engine.Reconcile` ran only in broker/sim, so one power pull mid LOOPS OFF fenced `journal.BrokerAccount` for good. That blocked LOOPS ON, budget raises, forget, change adoptions and owner grant intents.
  - After: `daemon.Run` starts `Engine.SettleUnknown` (broker/daemon/daemon.go:452). Every executor that answers from saved state now lifts its fence in the first round. Those executors are loops, change, grants gate, forget and recall rollback.
  - Executors that need a service retry from 2 s, doubling to 5 min. These are mail and follow.
- **Proportionate.**
  - The change is one 30-line loop (broker/journal/engine.go:494) and one wiring line, and it reuses `Reconcile` and `Resolve` unchanged.
  - It adds no package, dependency or new state.
  - Minimal for #1319.
- **Test #1319 asked for: present.**
  - `TestOP4StartupReconcileReleasesTheBrokerAccount` follows the path a power pull takes. It copies the journal while LOOPS OFF blocks in `Execute`, restarts, and makes evidence unavailable twice. It runs for both outcomes, applied and not applied.
  - It asserts that the fence lifts, LOOPS OFF lands in the right state, LOOPS ON dispatches, and only LOOPS ON executes.
  - `TestOP4UncertainOutcomeStaysFencedAndStopAnswers` covers the uncertain case. The account stays fenced, LOOPS ON is refused with `ErrUnreconciled`, and STOP over the owner channel replies and takes effect. A narrowing budget lower dispatches, and nothing re-runs.
- **Mutation probes (10 run; 3 killed, 2 equivalent, 5 survived).**
  - Killed:
    - M1: drop the `go eng.SettleUnknown` wiring.
    - M2: no retry, so one round only.
    - M7: re-dispatch an intent that reconcile settled `not_applied`. The applied=false case catches the re-execution.
  - Equivalent:
    - M4: drop the `ctx.Err()` exit, which the `select` on `ctx.Done` covers.
    - M8: let unknown fall through to `Resolve`, which refuses unknown.
  - Survived:
    - **M3.** Remove the 5 min cap (engine.go:512). The wait then doubles without bound: after about 17 failed rounds the next ask is days away.
    - **M10.** Every wait is `maxWait`.
    - **M5.** Drop the one-minute round bound (engine.go:499).
    - **M6.** Drop the `wait <= 0` guard. The daemon never passes 0.
    - **M9.** Start the loop on `context.Background()`. It then outlives the daemon and commits to a closed store.
- **L1 (later, recheck).** Pin the pacing and the loop's lifetime:
  - M3 and M10: assert the wait sequence or count asks under a cap.
  - M5: an executor that blocks past the round bound is asked again.
  - M9: the loop stops when the daemon's ctx ends.
  
  This is the recurring kind "a defence can be reverted and the suite still passes". The cap is what keeps a mail outage of an hour from becoming a fence of days.
- **R1 (release; shared with UX and L3).** The follow executor never settles a switch that did not land, so a power pull mid-follow still fences the broker account forever. See the UX record.
- **Overlap.** There is no file overlap with #1269, #1309, #1302 or #1323. #1269 edits `cmd/agentosd/main.go`, which this PR does not touch. The PR merges cleanly onto main 37fe6520.
