## Findings
- **R1, release (round 1; fixed in the delta).** Tools had no HOME, TMPDIR or USER and `/tmp` was read-only, so `mktemp`, `git config --global`, Go's GOCACHE and npm caches failed. The delta sets `include_only = ["PATH", "HOME", "TMPDIR"]` with managed HOME and TMPDIR in the workspace. Measured in a real tool shell: `mktemp` gives `/var/lib/agentos-work/tmp.*`, `git config --global` writes `$HOME/.gitconfig`, and Python's tempfile uses the workspace; a hostile lower layer's HOME, LD_PRELOAD and canary still do not reach the tool.
- **R2, release (round 1; fixed).** The carried measurements (runsc, C1-C15, within-session changes) were not recorded on #809; they now are.
- **L1, later (delta).** HOME and TMPDIR are the workspace root, so caches, `.gitconfig` and mktemp files land next to the task's output, and a repository's `.bashrc` runs in every tool shell (inside the sandbox). Owner: #807's wiring.
