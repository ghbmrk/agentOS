- **Gain.** It closes F12's gap: the vault process can now make the box's vault from the confirmed Owner Card (the step-3 seed carries over) and can discard a half-made one. It also unblocks card rotation (K19), which until now always answered "I keep no Owner Card".
- **No cap.**
  - The fresh data key costs the owner nothing.
  - The no-op reset on a setup vault no card made keeps the step-3 seed on every unlatched start.
  - Provision holds the custody lock for about 1 s (two KDFs) once at setup.
- **Not yet usable end to end.** #1198 (the setup vault is never opened) and R1 (nothing remakes the setup vault after a reset) are both release items; R1 is folded into #1198.
- **Proportionate.** It reuses `vault.CreateSealed`, `recovery.Provision` and `durable.Rename`, and adds no new component.
