This is the tier A lens screen, Potency pass. Issue #1271 is the brief (no `briefs/` file).

- **Gain.**
  - Before this PR, finish's seal checked only that a code generator was confirmed. A vault remade under a stale card latch (K22) could be sealed, and setup would finish with a card that opens nothing. That breaks A1 and recovery, and the owner would not know.
  - Now `sealEnroll` refuses with 428 while the open vault holds no `recovery.CardName` entry. The vault is the only party that knows whether a card made it.
- **Proportionate.**
  - One check in `sealEnroll`, placed after the not-confirmed check (broker/cmd/agentos-egress/enroll.go:182).
  - One status, added to the handler's fixed-refusal case.
  - One mapping per layer: `modelroute.ErrCardRequired`, then `localsrv.EnrollCardRequired`, then the existing `localapi.ErrCardPending`.
  - It reuses `enrollCall`'s status switch and the `unlockErr` pattern. No new dependency, package or op.
  - A distinct status is needed because 412 and 409 are taken. That matches how `enrollCall` already tells refusals apart.
- **Normal path completes.** With `ensureCard` made a no-op (M12), the tests that seal after a real `provisionCard` still pass (cardvault_test.go:344, setupopen_test.go:333 and :469). Only the rigs that never provision fail. So the production provision (`recovery.Provision`, recovery/rotate.go:129) writes the entry the seal checks.
