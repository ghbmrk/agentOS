Verdict: accept

Tier A, Potency lens only. Reviewed from the diff against its merge-base with `origin/main` and DEP-2a–c (briefs/DEP-2.md). Head unchanged since the L3 accept; CI green. Question: does moving the evidence to pipes and mounting kept paths read-only cap what the audit can run, or what it lets the project learn?

- Capability kept. Scenarios still get a fresh writable work directory (`tmp/`, `home/`, build caches), so builds and `HOME`-scoped installs work; only writes into `ROOT`, `sys.prefix` and keep entries now fail with EROFS. The PR's full `depaudit run` (controls plus both A9 scenarios) exits 0 and CI's `dependency-audit` is green, so no shipped scenario relied on those writes. Product scenarios cannot declare `writes`; ASSUMPTIONS D4 names the escape hatch (a schema change with its own review) and why it carries state. That cap buys matching security, so it is not proposed for lifting.
- Compounding gained. Per-target `attempts`, `faults` and `cleanup_retries` in the report and run line make HK-1's D1 and D3 observable over time, which is the input a later decision on the retry bound or on `STRACE_ATTEMPTS` needs.
- Throughput unchanged: same attempt bound, no extra process per attempt; the trace now travels in memory through `_run_sandboxed`'s captured stdout instead of a file, bounded by the narrow `TRACED` set.
- Considered, not proposed: letting product scenarios keep writable caches across attempts for speed. It would reopen the cross-attempt channel DEP-2b closes (invariant-adjacent, A9).
