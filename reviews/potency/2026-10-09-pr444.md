Tier A, Potency lens only. Reviewed from the diff against its merge-base with `origin/main`, CH-1, CH-7, CH-19, localui ASSUMPTIONS L31 and the L3 accept on #444. Question: does the control restore the owner channel after a SIM change, and what still caps it?

- It restores the channel, and that is a real gain. Before this, a swapped or unbound owner line paused texts with the owner for good: nothing wrote `owner_iccid`, so the only fix was editing a root-owned file. Now the owner recovers from the page with one code, and the bridge picks it up at its next open (at most 10 s) without a restart. Every text-based capability (approvals, codes, digests, chat) depends on this line, so a dead end here zeroed them all.
- Reversing the brief's direction (the bridge reports and agentosd records) keeps the bridge's roles directory read-only and adds no bridge op. That costs no capability and avoids a write path in the less-trusted process. No point.
- Security cost: none taken from an invariant; the always-a-code rule is the CH-19 price. UX cost: one code per SIM change.

Points:
1. **release (proposed P2-1 row, PR Findings)**: until the image makes `/var/lib/agentos/modem` writable by agentosd's user and readable by `agentos-modem`, `Record` fails on a real box and the gain above is zero. The builder proposed the row but left it for the coordinator; it is not on BOARD.md at this head. Add it before P2-1 closes, with an e2e check that an adopt reaches the bridge's next open.
2. **release (same defect as L3 point 1)**: a recorded adoption the bridge never takes leaves `adopted` set and hides the form, so the owner channel stays dark until agentosd restarts, with no retry. Expiring `adopted` after a few bridge reopen periods turns a silent dead end into a retry. Same row as L3 point 1 and UX point 2.
3. **later**: on a fresh box the owner line is unbound until the owner finishes setup, signs in, opens More and adopts the SIM, and setup records no modem roles (PR body: overlap with c2 checked). Offering the same adopt step at the end of setup, where the owner has just shown a code, would save one round trip on every new box. Weigh it when P2-2w c3 or the setup texts are next touched.
