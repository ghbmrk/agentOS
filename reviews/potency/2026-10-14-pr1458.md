## Round 1 (57868e73)

- **G1 working uplink: yes, over Ethernet.**
  - `Type=ether` + `Kind=!*` + `DHCP=yes` matches a physical port and nothing virtual. I checked systemd's negative-glob semantics: a NULL kind passes `!*`, and `veth` fails it.
  - The CI boot proves the positive match on virtio-net.
  - `agentosd`'s `routeOnline` (default route from `/proc/net/route` and `ipv6_route`) is what the page's "I plugged in an Ethernet cable" button checks, so a networkd DHCP default route turns it online with no restart.
  - `agentos-egress` learns the network through `netguard.WatchNetlink` plus its poll (`broker/cmd/agentos-egress/netguard.go:16`), so dropping `network-online.target` is safe.
  - Nothing else in the image (the package list is minimal) orders on `network-online.target`.
  - Nothing counts failed units (no `is-system-running` or `--failed` in `image/` or `broker/`), so the wait-online failure in #1457 u1a is indeed cosmetic.
- **Cable plugged in later:** networkd handles carrier changes, egress follows netlink, and the page polls the route table. The boot is not delayed, and the boot report does not wait when no port has carrier. This holds.
- **Wi-Fi-only homes:** G1 still cannot get online without a cable. That path needs #936 (station device, NM and polkitd), #1428 (join mediated by the loader) and #1429 (an honest error), and all three are open release items. This is by design for #1416. **P-1 (release, no new issue):** the G1 run plan must state "wired uplink" as a precondition. Until #1429 lands, a Wi-Fi-only owner sees "Check the password and try again".
- **Station decision on #1416:** sound. NM gets only the station, every other interface is unmanaged, and NM must keep `Requires=`/`After=agentos-inbound.service`. The image test fails if any `.network` file matches a radio, so the two configurations cannot overlap silently. It must still be read together with #1428 (no station join that bypasses `APLoader`).
- **P-2 (release): the boot report can print `none` with a cable in.**
  - `boot-report` gives up immediately when no physical port reads `carrier=1`.
  - It is ordered after health, bless and drive-id, but not after networkd bringing the link up plus autonegotiation. A real 1G or 2.5G PHY often needs 2 to 4 s after link-up before carrier reads 1, and `carrier` is unreadable while the link is still administratively down.
  - virtio's carrier is instant, so CI cannot see this.
  - On the M6 Ultra a fast boot can print `agentos-uplink: none` while the box goes online seconds later. That is exactly the evidence hardware check (a) in the new row reads.
  - Fix: give a short carrier grace (about 5 s) whenever a physical Ethernet port exists, and treat an unreadable `carrier` as "not yet" rather than "no cable".

## Delta round (038d484c)

- The change is `[ -n "$wired" ] || [ "$i" -le 5 ] || break`:
  - With no carrier, the loop sleeps at most 5 times, then reports `none`.
  - Once a port has carrier, the existing 30-iteration bound still applies.
  - Carrier is checked again before the break, so a carrier that rises during the fifth sleep is still seen.
- An unreadable carrier counts as "not yet": `cat` fails, its output is not `1`, and the port is skipped. This matches the kernel returning EINVAL while a link is down.
- `test_boot_report_waits_for_carrier_on_a_cold_nic` covers a carrier that is unreadable, then 0, then 1, and the lease is reported after 3 sleeps. `test_boot_report_waits_briefly_without_a_cable` bounds the no-carrier case to at most 5 sleeps.
- Nit (later): a box with no physical port also waits 5 s. My earlier draft asked for no wait in that case. Nothing orders after `agentos-boot-report.service`, so this only moves the console line.
