This is the tier A lens screen, Potency pass.

- **Gain.** Setup's network step (ONB-3) gets a backend. Until now `JoinNetwork` always failed. The right to write NM system connections stays out of agentosd and the page (L8).
- **Proportionate.**
  - One vendored, dependency-free module (godbus v5.1.0, D-108). An in-tree D-Bus client would be a larger, riskier surface.
  - The helper's socket reuses `sockets.Server`.
  - The NM seam has six methods, each one call.
- **No cap.** One join at a time, `busy` otherwise. The wait is fixed at 25 s and agentosd's call at 30 s.
- **Not yet usable end to end.** The image wiring (user, unit, polkit rule and `-netjoin-sock`) belongs to P2-1/c4 (N1). Without the station device from #936, every join answers `saved` after the full 25 s, and c4's `Online` (#931) is where a bad password surfaces.
- **Defences pinned.** Mutants that drop the peer check, the bounds check, the rate limit or the setup-open gate each fail a test.
- **L2 (later, recheck).** The helper's D-Bus calls run under the server's context with no deadline. godbus sets no default reply timeout. Only the poll loop is bounded by `Wait`. Filed as #1216.
  - If NM stalls on a call, the Joiner's mutex is held and every later join answers `busy` until the helper restarts.
  - If the lookup and write take more than about 5 s, agentosd's 30 s call ends before the helper answers. The page then shows its error for a connection that was saved.
  - Fix: one deadline, `Wait` plus a margin, on the whole `Join`, and `CallTimeout` derived from it.
- **Recurring kind, seen again.** "A brief's declared scope omits the test files the change forces" (Potency README: #628, #629, both `daemon/inference_test.go`). Here the change also forced `arc2_test.go`, `childproc/gate_test.go`, `core/core.txt` and `localapi/localapi_test.go`. The check is already proposed: `tools/doclint.py` scope comparison.

Owed: none from Potency.
