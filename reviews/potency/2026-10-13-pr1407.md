## Round 1 at 2cc2101 (fix-list)

Head 2cc2101d652f38b5c6cfe06845b3de1ce2f50af2.

This is the tier A lens screen, Potency pass. G1 means the controller boots, the phone controls it, and one useful model-backed task runs on the M6 Ultra (D-104). Installation and pairing follow the preloaded path (D-105), with the sticker's setup Wi-Fi (D-112).

- **Gain.**
  - The image now has an access point loader. It takes every radio down first (DEV-net-h), then serves the sticker's network on wlan0 at 10.42.0.1/24, and burns that network once the card is confirmed or the box is owned.
  - The modem bridge and the netjoin helper ship, with agentosd's `-netjoin-sock` pointed at the helper's socket.
  - The wiring for #1388 is right, and the image test checks it against the FlagSets.
- **Proportionate.** Debian's hostapd and dnsmasq do the radio and DHCP work. The new root command is needed by localui L15.
- **P-1 (blocker).**
  - `run` reads `/sys/class/net` once (`main.go:158-174`). If wlan0 is not there yet, it idles forever, and the unit has no dependency on the wlan0 device.
  - `mt7921e` probing and firmware loading during udev coldplug can easily come after that read, which leaves G1 with no setup network.
  - Fix: device activation (`BindsTo`/`After`/`WantedBy=sys-subsystem-net-devices-wlan0.device`), or exit/poll when the radio is missing. Add a test where the radio appears later.
- **P-2 (release, the builder's item).** `firmware-mediatek` (non-free-firmware) is not in the image (`Repositories=main`), so the M6 Ultra has no wlan0. File it as a G1 gate (filed as #1415).
- **P-3 (release, new).** No uplink is configured: no NetworkManager and no networkd `.network` file. Ethernet gets no address, so §8.1 step 5 and step 6 cannot complete on G1. The "install row" that I11 and I17 refer to does not exist (filed as #1416).
- **P-4 (release, #1185).**
  - After the confirm, a restart leaves no access point at all, which strands the steps after the card and leaves an owned box without CH-7's access point for good.
  - While the loader keeps running, the sticker network stays live after the confirm (D-112).
  - Widen #1185's text.
- **P-5 (later).** Note on #1185 that S-l1 and most of S-R3 landed here.
- **#936.** It still blocks the home Wi-Fi leg: one radio, no station device, and no NetworkManager or polkitd in the image. The PR says so.
