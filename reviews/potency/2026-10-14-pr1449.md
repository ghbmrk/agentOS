## Findings
- A large tree adopted in loop 1 now reaches the guest. `guest/mcp.go` `m.result` folds it, the bridge sees `!Unchanged && Version==""`, calls `result_read`, and `mirror` applies it. Later polls get the small `unchanged` answer: no refetch and no new fold record. A tree over `machineBytes` (4 MiB) is handed back whole and fits under `maxTreeAnswer` (8 MiB).
- The refusal guard is sound against agentosd: a real non-unchanged answer always has a non-empty version (ASSUMPTIONS row 1 holds). If the record is evicted between Hand and Read, the bridge refuses, keeps its tree, and recovers on the next poll.
- P-1 (later): a persistent `mirror` failure leaves `version` unset. Every 30 s poll refetches the tree and the guest plane stores a new fold record, so after about 32 polls (about 16 minutes) the agent's own folded results are evicted.
- P-2 (later): the tree's fold record stays in the machine's fold store after the bridge has read it. With a 4 MiB budget per machine, a tree of a few MiB evicts most of the agent's folded results at each tree change.
