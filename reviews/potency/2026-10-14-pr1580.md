Tier A. Cited: REC-4. Question asked: does the new result expiry keep its leverage (a late confirm gets its done page) without lifting the seed's lifetime, and do the tests hold both?

## Findings
- **P1, blocker (#1340 bound).** The "never more than `resultGrace` past the window" cap is not pinned. Run locally on the head: changing `r.until.Add(resultGrace)` to `r.until.Add(2*resultGrace)` passes every `TestRotation*` test. Case "capped at the window plus the grace" confirms at 40m, after the 30m cap, and reads 1s later; a cap of 40m expires at the same instant. Failure path: a longer cap keeps the seed in memory up to 5 minutes longer than L46 says and no test fails. Fix: add a case confirming inside the cap's reach (for example confirm at 33m, read at 35m+1s, not shown; and read at 35m-1s, shown).
- **P2, blocker (#1340 contract: "the later of the window's end and 5 minutes after the answer").** The window-extension branch (`if until.Before(r.until) { until = r.until }`) has no test. Deleting it passes every `TestRotation*` test, because no case confirms early and reads after 5 minutes but before the window ends. Failure path: an owner who confirms at 1m of a 10m window and opens the done page 6m later is sent Home; today's behaviour (the window) is lost. Fix: one case, window 10m, confirm at 1m, read at 6m, shown.
- Both fixes are cases in `TestRotationLateConfirmKeepsTheResultForAGrace`; no code change. This is the recurring kind "a defence can be reverted and the suite still passes" in [README](README.md).
