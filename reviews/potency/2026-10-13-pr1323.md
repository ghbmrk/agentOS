Tier A lens screen, Potency pass. The question: does the change cap what the owner or the box can do without buying matching security or UX?

- **What the owner keeps.** REC-4 rotation for any subset of the five parts is unchanged: start, display, confirm, done, enroll, show. The confirming phone still lands on `/rotate/done` and sees its notes and the new code generator once.
- **What is narrowed.** A second signed-in phone or browser can no longer open the result. No REC-4 or OWN flow needs that. The code generator, the only part of the result that cannot be recomputed, is still reachable from any signed-in session through U-R1 of #1175: the start page's "Show the new code generator" form, with the new recovery key, while enrollment is pending. So the narrowing costs the owner nothing they cannot get back with the card they just printed.
- **No new component, no new op.** It reuses `cookieToken`/`tokenKey` and the display's own handler. agentosd and localapi are untouched (no op-count change), and ARC-2 is unaffected.

Considered, not proposed: letting any of the owner's sessions fetch the notes until the window ends. That reopens #1316. The recovery-key re-display already covers the case of a phone that switched.
