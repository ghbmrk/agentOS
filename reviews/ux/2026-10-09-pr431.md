Tier A, UX lens only; Security is stage 4a. Reviewed only the PR's own changes, from its merge-base 94ce0df (the head merges main), with CH-3, CH-7, CH-10, CH-11, CH-18 and localui L25. Question: what does the owner see when a lock overlaps a page sign-in or RESUME?

- Ordinary sign-in, refresh, STOP and RESUME are unchanged, and there is no new prompt. That matches the brief's cost line.
- When a lock overlaps a sign-in, the page gets a token that dies on first use and sends the owner back to sign-in (LATER SR3-1 f2). This is rare and fails safe.

Points:
1. **later (CH-11, CH-12)**: when a lock lands between the token check and the commit of a page RESUME, agentosd now answers ErrUnauthorized, and `localui` maps any error on RESUME to "RESUME failed to record. Still stopped." "Still stopped" is true, but "failed to record" suggests a fault, not a lock, and the owner is not told to sign in again. Proposal: map ErrUnauthorized on /resume to the unlock page, as Approvals and Paused already do. This only happens in a race of a few seconds, so it is not a blocker.
