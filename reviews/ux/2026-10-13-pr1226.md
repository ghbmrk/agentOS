This is the tier A lens screen, UX pass.

- **New owner-facing text.** All of it is first person, plain and GSM-7 (`TestTheCardStepCarriesTheRestartNote` checks the two notes):
  - the claim busy answer: "I am busy checking codes. Try again in a moment.", with `Retry-After: 5`;
  - the codes step's replace note, which names the "AgentOS" entry, matching the otpauth issuer;
  - the card step's restart note, which closes LATER CTRL-card l2.
- **Flow.**
  - A confirm refused for an unconfirmed code generator now lands on the codes step with the note, rather than the generic retry page.
  - The display keeps the same card, so a card the owner already printed stays good after step 3.
  - A reset that remade the vault sends the next `/setup` to the codes step.
- **L2 (later).** The 503 busy view drops the typed 16-symbol claim code, so under a flood the owner retypes it on every retry. Carry `typed` back, since busy is not the owner's mistake. Drafted in CTRL-card-sock l2.
- **Minor, not filed.** The replace note also shows on a codes step that says codes are unavailable. That step already tells the owner to have the box prepared again.
- **R1 note.** Until CTRL-card-sock r1 lands, a preloaded box with the page's current unit would show the download route instead of the claim screen. The owner would never be asked for the sticker, and setup would stall later without a reason given.

Owed: none from UX.
