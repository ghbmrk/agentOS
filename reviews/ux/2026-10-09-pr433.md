Each term matches what `matches` enforces: an empty recipient list reads "any the source record names", a zero cap reads "no money may move", a zero hold reads "no hold". Points:
1. **later (ADP-9)**: "When: every field comes from the source record" contradicts the "Fixed field" lines below it, which the owner sets. Suggested wording: "the record and its recipients come from the source system".
2. **later (ADP-9)**: "Amount: up to 150000.00" names no currency and assumes two decimal places (`minor`), so it reads wrongly for a zero-decimal currency. This already happens in `Describe`. Fix both when an adapter reports its currency.
3. **later (ADP-9)**: the rule runs "without asking or notifying you", but the card does not say how to stop it. Add one fixed term that names the text command to pause or revoke the rule (CH-11) and says STOP halts it.
