This is the tier A lens screen, UX pass.

- **Against #1220.**
  - With a usable line and a known number, the number step is true: the real number, the `sms:` link, PAIR replies and the typing fallback.
  - With a down line, it says plainly that pairing is not available.
- **B1 (blocker; ONB-6, CH-12). The line can text, but the number is unknown.**
  - `BoxNumber()` answers "" (localui/setuphooks.go:131-135). The step then says "I cannot send texts right now. Reload this page to try again." (localui/pages.go:286) and hides "Enter your number instead".
  - That fallback would work. It needs only a line that can text, and the paired reply gives the owner the box's number.
  - The number comes only from `BOX_NUMBER` or `AT+CNUM` (modem/at/driver.go:261-269). Setup cannot set `BOX_NUMBER`, and many SIMs leave their own-number entry empty.
  - A1 then stops at step 2 and stays stopped, with a cause that is false and a step that cannot fix it.
  - Fix:
    - Separate "can text" from "number known".
    - When the line is ready but the number is unknown, show the fallback with a first-person line.
    - Test that state.
- **R3 (release; reclassed from the builder's later).** The allow-listed paired text is fixed to "this PC" (localapi/setup.go:97).
  - When #1221 serves `HostInfo`, every paired reply is refused, and both callers drop the error (localui/setup.go:752, setuphooks.go:171).
  - The owner pairs but never gets the reply from the box's number (§8.1).
- **R4 (release; CH-12).** The "not available" note names only "Reload".
  - When the SIM is not in the modem, or the modem is not plugged in, the owner loops on reload.
  - The setup line knows the state (down, unbound or swapped), so the note can name the step.
- **L4 (later; wording).** The send-failure text "Check the SIM, or use Text my box." (localui/setup.go:716) is now shown only on a ready line.
  - Setup's 5-a-minute or 20-an-hour cap is then the likeliest cause, and the text should name the wait.
  - The "AgentOS" spelling in the texts is #1262's. The allow-list already accepts "agentOS".
- **Owner effort.** On the working path the owner needs one tap and Send. No internal names reach the owner. The texts are GSM-7 and fit one segment.
