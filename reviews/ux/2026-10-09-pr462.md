Points:
1. **later (CH-12, truth of a wait text)**: `guest/plane.go` STATUS lines changed from "It has been told to free space" / "to flatten them" to "I'm freeing space" / "I'm flattening them". The broker only tells the agent. Whether space gets freed depends on the agent, so the new line claims something under way that may not be. "Nothing to do unless this lasts" limits the harm. Wording when the line is next touched, e.g. "I'm asked to free space", or name the owner step if it lasts. ("my files … they're full" was there before: the space is full, not the files.)
2. **later (CH-21, one voice on the setup page)**: setup step 2 (`localui/pages.go:258-263`) now uses "my number" for two different numbers. The link "Save my number as a contact" (the box's voice, the box's number) sits directly above "Enter my number instead" (the owner's voice, the owner's number), under the heading "Text your box" and the button "Text my box" (SPEC §8.1's name). An owner can save or enter the wrong number. The page needs one voice for its labels, e.g. "Save this number as a contact" or "Enter your number instead". The button name is in SPEC, so this belongs in the UX wording pass (LATER CH-21c f3) or an L1 spec diff. It would be a release point, but the summary line is not in the diff and a wrong pick here only fails pairing visibly.
3. **later (CH-12, existing before this PR)**: `localui/vault.go` fallback "I refused that. Try again." gives no cause, and trying again may not fix it. The first person makes it read as a deliberate refusal. Map it to the cause, or say what to check, when vault texts are next touched.
