# UX, PR #1044 (MAIL-gen)

Record: PR #1044 · package MAIL-gen · head 73109a9

**Scope:** owner-visible text when an intent is refused after the owner replaces the mailbox. I traced the reachability from the guest submit and the owner's YES, through the gate.

**What holds**
- The vault already tells the owner on replacement: "The mail account was replaced on my Wi-Fi page." (`noteMailReplaced`).
- The new refusals add no identifier or hash to any owner or guest text. `errMailGen` reaches the log only, and the poll's log lines are fixed words.

**Findings**
- **U-R1 release** (no new row: add to SR3-mail-w2-f1 precondition (a); CH-12; recurring kind "a line gives no cause or next step").
  - Problem: an owner who answers YES to a mail ask after replacing the mailbox gets "Approved K7." and then nothing. `settle` → `Authorize` re-evaluates, the stamp mismatches and the intent is Denied. `ownerVerdict` returns no verdict for an approved-then-denied intent, so no line follows.
  - Failure path: the owner believes a reply went out that never did.
  - Fix: when the recheck of an owner-approved mail intent is refused for an account change, send one line, for example "K7 did not run: the mail account was replaced after I asked. Ask your agent again if still needed." This mirrors the gate's existing "did not run: it changed after my Wi-Fi page showed it" line.
- **U-L1 later.** The replacement notice could say what happens to open work, for example "Mail actions asked before this will be refused; your agent will ask again." It could also close open mail asks at once, so the owner is not left to answer asks that can no longer run.

Findings: blocker 0; release 1; later 1.
