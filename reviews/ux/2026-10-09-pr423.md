Tier A, UX lens only; Security is stage 4a. Reviewed from the diff against its merge-base with main, UPD-5, CH-12, CH-13, change C25, maintain M8 and grants GR27. Question: when an update ask closes without the owner's answer, does the owner see a sensible next ask?

- Yes. An ask that expired, was voided or was dropped is asked again at the next update check, at most a day later, under a new request (C25, M8). Before this change it stayed silent until a restart. NO and a partial YES are not re-asked, so the owner is not nagged about a choice already made.
- GR27, the call deferred to this lens: keep "Nothing is needed." in the page-change notice for a pipeline release. It is true, it fits one segment (CH-12), and a promise such as "It will be offered again" would be false after a NO elsewhere, a newer release or a pinned channel. No text change.

Points:
1. **release (UPD-5, CH-12; recurring kind "a problem text names a step that cannot work")**: after the owner's NO or a partial YES that leaves a release out, Loop 3 keeps the version as awaiting the owner until a restart. STATUS keeps saying "Update N has been waiting for your approval since …" when no request is open, so the owner cannot act on it. This was already true for every unanswered close before this PR, which narrows it to NO and "not chosen". Fix in the next package that touches Loop 3 STATUS: a version whose proposal the pipeline dropped without a lapse should read as declined or not chosen, not as waiting.
2. **later (UPD-5)**: an owner who never answers is asked again once per check, indefinitely. That is acceptable for a security release, but after a few lapses the ask could move to the daily digest only.
