What the owner sees after a 409 or no answer when the hand-off fails (HTTP 410, plain text):

> Part of your new card is already in effect, but I could not finish. Keep the new card, and your current one if you still have it. Finish now: start a new card from my box page, using the passphrase or recovery key from whichever card works. My Wi-Fi may still use your old card's password: I could not switch it. What works:
- The failure is now visible.
- "may" is honest, because the write can land even when the reply is lost.
- The note is a fixed string and the test checks that no password appears in it.
- It shows again on a later GET.
- The confirm page still warns before the switch.
- Treating `ErrDirSync` as switched removes a false "still old" message on the done page.

- **U-1, later.** `broker/localui/rotwire.go:257`: "the new card you start replaces the Wi-Fi password too" holds after a 409, where the owed parts are enforced (`recovery/rotate.go:273`). It is not enforced after no answer when the vault never stored the commit, or committed it cleanly: nothing is then owed, and the owner can start a grid-only card. Suggested wording: "...; when you start the new card, include the Wi-Fi password." Alternatively, have the rotation form pre-tick Wi-Fi.

The phone drops off within one poll (2 s), and the in-effect text tells the owner to keep both cards, so an owner who rejoins may try the old card's QR first. Add the clean path's "My Wi-Fi now uses your new card's password ... rejoin with the Wi-Fi QR code on your new card" there too.
