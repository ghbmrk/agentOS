## #363 P2-2a f2: a changed release adoption says nothing is needed (b75d319)
**Verdict: accept.**
- [Fact] Owner-facing text: for a `meta.change.*` intent with Origin `change` that changed after the page showed it, the notice now ends "Nothing is needed." instead of "Make the request again if still needed." Every other origin keeps the f1 wording. CH-12 allows exactly this ending ("says what the owner can do, or that nothing is needed"), and the old ending named a step the owner could not take (there was no request to make). This closes one instance of the README's recurring kind "a problem text names a step that cannot work".

- [Fact] GR27 and the gate comment state the limit correctly. `maintain.New` drops `StateAwaitingOwner` entries from `Proposed` on load, so a restart re-offers the version.
