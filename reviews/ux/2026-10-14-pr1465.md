## UX
**Rotation of Wi-Fi, setup or grid with no slot, and the save fails.** `Commit` returns a plain error. The egress process drops the rotation and answers 410: "no new card is waiting; nothing changed, start again". The display discards the new card. Before this PR, that message could be false: the first `Put` might have landed. Now "nothing changed" is true, apart from L3-2 below. To retry, the owner starts a new rotation.

**Rotation with a slot, and the card save fails.** The ErrCardNotStored path is unchanged. The owner gets 409, the display goes to ErrInEffect, and the note says "print the new card shown and replace the parts it owes". The owed parts are still recorded by `markRotation`.

**Later:** the 410 text does not say that a save failed, so a disk or TPM fault reads like a lapse. Not a regression. It could be folded into the next rotation UX pass.
