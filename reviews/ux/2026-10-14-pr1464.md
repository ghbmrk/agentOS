## UX
- **Later U1: the timeout isn't named in the log.** When the 5 s cap is hit, the only line is `killExec`'s `gvisor: <id>: killing cancelled command <pid>: runsc kill: signal: killed: ...`. A timeout surfaces as `signal: killed` (checked with go1.24), with no "timed out after 5s". `Kill` logs nothing about having waited or for how long, and a slow kill that succeeds leaves no trace. An operator who sees a slow Rollback can't tie it to this wait. A suggested fix: `killExec` checks `ctx.Err()` and logs `timed out after 5s`, and `Kill` logs `waited <d> for a cancelled command's kill` when the wait passes about 1 s.
