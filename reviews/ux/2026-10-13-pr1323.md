Verdict: accept

Tier A lens screen, UX pass. No owner-facing text changed. The behaviour that changed is when `/rotate/done` shows the result: only to the confirming session, only before the confirm window ends, and not after `/rotate/enroll`, `/rotate/show` or a new start. In every other case it sends the browser to `/home`.

- **Happy path unchanged.** The owner confirms, gets a 303 to `/rotate/done` on the same phone, and sees the notes and the QR. Enroll and show re-render the done page themselves, so voiding the stored result there loses nothing the owner was not already looking at.
- **The session changes mid-rotation.** A Wi-Fi rejoin, including the "Rejoin with the new card" case after a Wi-Fi rotation, keeps the phone's cookie for the same host. The binding survives, and the reconnect case works. The binding breaks only if the phone gets a new session between the confirm and the done page: it signed in again, the cookie was cleared, or it reached the box under another host name. The owner then lands on Home with no word, as any second session does. They lose the notes, which advise backing up, destroying the old grid, and the lost-card advice. The code generator stays recoverable from Replace Owner Card parts with the new recovery key (#1175 U-R1), and the done page's own text already points there. So this is a quiet loss of advice, not a stuck owner: see U-l1.
- **U-R1 (release; REC-4, recurring kind "a problem text names a step that cannot work" adjacent).** The result expires at the rotation's confirm window (`r.until`, from Start), not at a time measured from the commit. A confirm made near the end of the window commits the new card, then sends the owner to Home with no notes and no QR.
  - Window. The vault window is 30 minutes from the start (`PendingTTL`). A recovery-key commit may take up to `commitTimeout` (2 min) to re-encrypt.
  - Plausibility. The owner may well spend most of that window printing and filing the new card.
  - Reproduction. A 10-minute window, confirm at 9m59s, then GET `/rotate/done` 2 s later: a 303 to `/home`.
  - Fix. Keep the result for the confirming session for a short grace after the commit answers, for example `max(r.until, commitTime+5m)`, still capped. Expiring with the window serves the threat of an unfetched seed lingering; a few minutes after commit, for the binding session alone, does not reopen #1316. Security should agree the grace.
- **U-l1 (later).** When `/rotate/done` has nothing for this session (another phone, expired, already shown or voided), the page redirects to Home silently. A one-line flash such as "Your new card is in effect. Its notes were shown once on the phone that confirmed it; Replace Owner Card parts shows the new code generator again with your recovery key." would tell the owner what happened and the step.

Considered, not proposed: re-showing the notes from the vault process. That needs a new op, and the notes are derivable from the parts the owner chose.
