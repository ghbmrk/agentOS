## Threat model
- Fold id pointed at attacker content: no. The stand-in comes from the broker, `fold.Store.Read` is scoped to the machine, and ids are 128-bit random. There is no sha256 check, but the broker is trusted.
- Path traversal: no. The read-back goes through the unchanged `mirror` (IsLocal, Clean, namespace allow-list, os.Root).
- Unbounded fetch or memory: no. At most one `result_read` per poll, an 8 MiB LimitReader on both calls, and a fold record of at most 4 MiB.
- Other deletion paths: none. A truncated read, a read error, a missing version or `unchanged` are all refused before `mirror`.
- Trust boundary unchanged: no broker code changes, and `result_read` is already a guest tool.
