**S1 (blocker, CAP-3 acceptance 2, defect).** The stale-approval check in `executeRenew` (`!ok || !first.Equal(since) || !r.full(lineage)`, renew.go:127) can be replaced by `if false` and the whole suite still passes. Dropping only the `!r.full(lineage)` term also passes. The only stale case tested is a second YES on the same `since`, and `Reach.TakeBack`'s own done-guard hides that case. Failure path: the owner asks, then the lineage stops being full (a deletion reach, or a raised cap), and a later YES still resets its machines from `since`. Fix: a test that makes the lineage not full between the ask and the YES (for example `x.r.prov.SetLimits(Limits{Reads: 5})`) and checks that there are no machine calls and that the stale text is sent. Also assert the stale text on the existing duplicate-YES path. This is the potency README's "defence revert survives" kind.

No other findings. The carried-renewal completion text (#1167) is about wording and owner experience, not a way around the defence.
