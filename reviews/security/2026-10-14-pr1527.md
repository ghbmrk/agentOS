## Findings
- **B1, blocker (round 1; fixed in delta).** After an unsynced landed write, `advance` raised the counter before the next write, so a power cut could leave the file at n against a counter at n+1 (ErrRolledBack, recovery-key restore). `bind` and `advance` now sync the vault directory (`syncDir`) before the catch-up `Increment`, and return or bind without incrementing if it fails.
- **R1, release (round 1; fixed in delta).** Anchor's re-anchor and Define-failure writes without `landed` (M10, M11) and the PutMany old-value wipe (M12) were unpinned; all three are now killed.
- **R2, release (round 1; fixed in delta).** D2 overstated recall; it now names journal and recall `FileStore.Rewrite` and cites #1318 for `DirStore`. The OnWarn-reaches-only-the-log half is in #1541.
- **N2, note (delta, not filed).** On Linux a later successful directory fsync after EIO does not by itself prove the earlier rename durable; ext4 and xfs go read-only on a failed journal commit. Outside the vault seam.
- **CI (round 1).** checks timed out in the Codex bwrap test; filed as #1542.
