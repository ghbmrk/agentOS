## Review (summary)

- **Overlapping full reads (the #1439 mechanism):** fixed. `netGuard` starts one `WatchEventsEvery`, which feeds netlink events and ticks into one `Watch`; its single refresher goroutine serialises `Refresh`. The only other `Refresh` callers are the synchronous start reads in egress and holder, before any worker. The stale branch (policy.go:255-258) therefore cannot regress External.
- **Dropped or coalesced events:** no lost wake-up. Each hop (netlink chan, `ev`, `kick`) drops a signal only while a token is already buffered, and that token is consumed after the change.
- **Flood:** the forward to `ev` is non-blocking and `select` is random over ready cases, so a netlink flood cannot block the ticker or the worker. Same as main.
- **Closed or failed feed:** a closed feed sets `events = nil` and the poll keeps running; a netlink open error passes nil and the poll alone applies. Both pinned by tests.
- **Shutdown:** `WatchEventsEvery` returns after `Watch`, which returns after its refresher. **Read errors:** the last learned set is kept. **Holder:** `WatchEvery` becomes `WatchEventsEvery(ctx, nil, d)`, identical behaviour.
