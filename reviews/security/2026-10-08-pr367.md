- [Fact] The vault (K17) now splits finishing into two steps:
  - `confirmEnroll` writes the seed, then the `owner-totp-confirmed` mark, then deletes the pending seed. It no longer seals.
  - `POST /enroll/seal` (`sealEnroll`) seals only if setup is open, enrollment is not locked, there is no pending seed, and the confirmed mark exists. Otherwise it answers 409.

- [Fact] `localsrv.Setup`:
  - Its record is 0600, replaced atomically and loaded strictly. An unreadable record closes setup.

  - `finish` requires `Enrolled` and then the vault's seal. `EnrollClosed` is accepted. `EnrollNone` clears `Enrolled` and refuses.

  - The page holds no seed. It checks the otpauth link (≤512 characters, `otpauth://totp/` prefix, base32 secret of 16–64 characters) before drawing it.
