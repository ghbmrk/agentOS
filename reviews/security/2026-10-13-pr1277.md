**Threat check**
- **sysfs read (`broker/localui/station.go:14-29`)**: no injection path.
  - `netClass` is the constant `/sys/class/net` (`broker/cmd/agentos-localui/main.go:94`), and `ap` comes from the operator's `-iface` flag.
  - Entry names come from `os.ReadDir`, so they cannot contain `/`. They are joined only with the fixed `wireless` or `phy80211` and passed to `os.Stat`.
  - `os.Stat` follows the `/sys/class/net/<if>` symlinks. That is intended and only reaches kernel-owned sysfs. Nothing is opened, read or written, and nothing is executed.
- **Cost per page load (`broker/localui/setup.go:479`)**: one `ReadDir` plus at most 2 stats per interface, all in memory.
  - The `p.NeverOnline &&` short-circuit means the read runs only while never_online holds.
  - It runs under `u.mu`. Sysfs does not block, so this adds no lock that an unclaimed client can exploit beyond what it could already do by reloading `/setup`.
- **Fail closed (`broker/localui/station.go:16-18`)**: an unreadable directory counts as no station device, which gives the Ethernet note and no rejoin form.
  - Mutation `return true` on error: killed (`TestStationDevice`).
  - Mutation that drops the AP-interface skip (`station.go:20`): killed (`TestStationDevice`, `TestStationRereadsInterfaces`).
  - Nil `Station` keeps the c4 behaviour.
- **Setup and claim gating**: unchanged.
  - The `done()` redirect, `phone()`, the `claim`/`elsewhere` step overrides (`setup.go:470-475`), `mayLocked`, and the `/setup/network` POST route and its rate limit are all untouched.
  - The change only picks a note string from two fixed constants and can only remove the Rejoin form (`setup.go:523`). It adds no route, input or capability.
- **Info exposure to unclaimed AP clients**: the note's wording reveals one bit, whether a second Wi-Fi device exists, and only while never_online holds. That is hardware detail with no security value, shown inside the CH-7 pre-setup window that is already open. No interface names or sysfs contents reach the response.
- **Sandbox**: the PR changes no unit file. Reading `/sys/class/net` needs no new privilege.
