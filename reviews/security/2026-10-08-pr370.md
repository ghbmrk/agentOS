## Evidence

- [Fact] `go test -race -count=1 ./follow/ ./cmd/agentosd/ ./daemon/ ./grants/ ./localsrv/ ./localui/ ./localapi/` passes at 5c21fc2.
- [Fact] The gate's `evaluateFollow` admits a follow only when all of these hold:
  - `Origin` is local and the executor is `grants.FollowExecutor`.
  - There are no params, the nonce is 1–64 hex characters (`FollowID`/`FollowOf`) and the digest is hex.
  - The name is empty (switching back) or passes `followName`: printable, 1–40 runes, ≤4 digits, trimmed, and not starting "the AgentOS project" in any case.
  - The local UI is configured.

  It then asks the owner locally at the high tier, with `Detail: "root fingerprint xxxx-xxxx"`.
- [Fact] `follow.Executor.Execute`:
  - It runs only a root the page still holds, under its digest.
  - Switching back (no name) needs `Project(digest)`: the held root's keys must equal those of the root the image ships.
  - A failed store write when the root may already be in place answers `ResultUnknown`, and `Reconcile` decides.
- [Fact] The alert is written to `follow-alert-pending` (atomic) before it is sent. It is cleared only if nothing has replaced it. It is retried every minute and shown on STATUS through `Note` until sent.
- [Fact] The page:
  - The ask form's token is an HMAC over (session, digest, project) and is checked with `hmac.Equal`. A switch-back carrying a name is refused as stale.
  - The upload is capped with `MaxBytesReader`/`LimitReader`.
  - A refused root shows only one of three whitelisted coarse causes (`rootReason`).
- [Fact] `guardClock` answers the far future until the HOST-1b guard runs, so every root reads as expired (WF2). `daemon.Run` refuses a page that offers following without the broker executor.

## Threat check

Asset: the box's root of trust for updates (OSS-9/OSS-10), and the owner's knowledge of where updates come from. Adversaries:

- **F**: a fork or impostor source that supplies a root file and wording to the owner.
- **P**: a person on the box's Wi-Fi without the owner's sign-in.
- **G**: an agent or guest.
- **N**: someone who controls the box's clock source.
