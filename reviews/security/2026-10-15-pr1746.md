##### `Bridged()` (`modemlink.go:488-492`, `:574-582`, `:628`)

- **Races.** None. `polls` and `polled` are read and written only under `l.mu`, as are `l.out` and `Bridged`. The decrement and stamp sit in a `defer` that runs on every return path of `outbox`, including the ctx-ended one.
- **Can it report true forever (a stall)?** No. Each of its three terms is bounded:
  - `polls > 0`: `outbox` ends at `PollWait`, at the bridge's hang-up (owner ops are `HangupOps`, `daemon.go:433-436`), or at `sockCtx`.
  - `len(l.out) > 0`: `deliver` drops the item at `SendWait` (`modemlink.go:340-344`).
  - The `polled` stamp ages out after 1 s on a monotonic `time.Now`.

  Beyond that, the drain's own bound of 30 s still caps it.
- **Can it report false while a bridge is mid-handover?**
  - Not on the normal path. From the moment `outbox` hands out an item until `sent` arrives, `l.out` holds it. `sent` stamps `polled`, and the bridge polls again at once (`bridge.go:323-344`). Between empty polls the gap is a redial.
  - **One edge case:** see S9.
- **No new trust surface.** `Bridged` is a read-only, in-process accessor with no new op, socket or peer. A local peer that can reach `owner.sock` (bridge UID only) could keep the drain alive by polling. That holds the stop at most to the existing 30 s bound, the same as at 3500839.

##### New findings (non-blocking)

**S8. class:release: no PR test pins the hang-up path that the shipped-order guarantee relies on (mutant L survives).**
- **Where.** Every bridged drain test runs `PollWait: 100ms` (`drain_test.go:125`).
- **Effect.** If the owner ops stopped being `HangupOps`, a bridge killed mid-poll (the normal systemd case) would hold `polls > 0` for up to 25 s. The drain would then stall to nearly its bound, and every test would still pass.
- **Evidence.** My ad-hoc test above passes at 0.9 s on the head. Under mutant L it fails ("Wait held the stop 4s after a mid-poll hang-up").
- **Ask.** Add a third subtest to `TestShutdownDrainEndsWhenNoBridgeIsConnected` with the default `PollWait` and a poll in flight whose client context is cancelled before the stop. It can land here or in #1772.

**S9. class:release: `BridgeGap` (1 s, `modemlink.go:53`) equals the bridge's back-off after a failed poll (`modem/bridge/bridge.go:328-333`, 1 s plus the redial).**
- **Effect.** A connected bridge whose poll fails once during the drain (a refused connection, a decode error) counts as gone, and the queued alert is lost.
- **Not a regression.** That is the same outcome as main, and it only arises where the bridge outlives agentosd's SIGTERM, which the shipped units never do.
- **Ask.** When #1772 lands, set `BridgeGap` clearly above the bridge's back-off (for example 3 s), or stamp failed polls. Fold this into #1772.
