## Round 1 (4ca8171)

### What changes for an attacker
`agentos-netjoin` gains a second op, `scan`, on its one socket. It is read-only: NetworkManager's `Devices`, `DeviceType`, `GetAllAccessPoints`, `Ssid` and `Strength`, with no `RequestScan` and no polkit change. agentosd relays the result as `page_setup_networks` (`OpSetupNetworks`), and the page offers the names in a `<select>` beside a typed-name field.

| Path | Stop | How |
|---|---|---|
| Setup op after finish | structural | `page_setup_networks` is wrapped in `Setup.open`, is in `SetupOps`, and is served only in setup mode to the page's uid. Dropping `s.open` is killed. |
| Failure modes | fail closed | No helper, an error, a nil list or the limit each answers an empty list, and the page takes a typed name. `Client.Networks` errors on a malformed answer, unknown fields, more than 20 names or a non-`Showable` name. `AgentosdSetup.Networks` gives nil on an error or more than 20 names. |
| Another uid on the helper socket | structural, unchanged | `PeerUID` is checked before any read, with MaxConns 2. `scan` takes no args, `null` or `{}` only, and refuses others before any D-Bus call. |
| D-Bus reach (L8.5) | structural | The destination is fixed to NetworkManager. Paths must sit under `/Devices/` or `/AccessPoint/` (`under`). The calls are four property reads and one method, and there are no writes. `50-agentos-netjoin.rules` is unchanged. |
| Hostile SSID bytes | structural | `Showable` checks 1-32 bytes, valid UTF-8, and no Cc or Cf. It runs in the helper and in agentosd. Names go out as `html/template` option text and as `encoding/json` values. Worst-case answer is about 3.9 KB, under the 8 KiB read. |
| Info to unclaimed clients | accepted | Names only (CRED-1). They are seen only by clients on the setup AP, which takes the sticker/card PSK. SSIDs are public broadcast data. Nothing is logged (L8.6). |
| Box's own setup AP in the list | accepted (brief out of scope, N10) | Choosing it costs availability only, and `Online` progress shows it. No new secret exposure. |
| Confusable or evil-twin names | accepted | Adds nothing over the exact-SSID WPA2 twin that the typed path already faces. |
| Scan flooding | accepted | 20 a minute, `ScanWait` 3 s inside `ScanTimeout` 4 s. It degrades to the typed name. Only an AP-password holder can drive it (L8.8). |
