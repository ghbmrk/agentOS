## Draft issue: TREE-answer-bound (as drafted; filed as #1487, release)
The managed_tree size cap counts raw bytes, but the bridge limits the encoded answer.

agentosd's `liveTree.Call` caps a tree answer at `maxTreeSend` (4 MiB), counting `len(path)+len(content)`. The guest bridge reads the JSON-RPC answer through `io.LimitReader(…, maxTreeAnswer)` (8 MiB). The tree is JSON-encoded twice on the way: agentosd's marshal (base64 content, HTML-escaped strings), then the plane's `writeRPC` marshal of it as a text string. Ordinary trees stay under the limit (4 MiB of tiny files gives 7.2 MiB). Paths with `<`, `>` or `&` grow about 7x per character, though. A probe with 4,194,240 counted bytes produced a 19,014,008-byte answer. The bridge's decode then fails, the tree is refused and kept stale, and every 30 s poll refetches the whole answer.

This existed before PR #1484: the same answer used to come back through `result_read`, or whole when it was over machineBytes. It fails closed. Fix options: (a) measure `len(json-encoded answer)` against a limit derived from `maxTreeAnswer` in agentosd, so a tree too large to deliver is reported as too large; (b) restrict tree paths to a safe character set at adoption (change/apply).
Test: a tree with escaped-character paths that is under 4 MiB of raw bytes is either delivered whole or refused by agentosd with "too large", never truncated at the bridge.
Serves CAP-5, A11. Failure path: an adoption never reaches the guest, the agent keeps serving the old skills, and the only sign is a log line in the guest.
