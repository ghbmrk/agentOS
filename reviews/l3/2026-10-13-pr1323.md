- **#1316's acceptance is met.**
  - Bound to the confirming session: `serveCard` sets `confirmer` under `postMu`, and `provision` stores `resOwner`.
  - Expiry with the confirm window: `r.until`, capped at `resultTTL`.
  - Cleared by enroll, show and Start: `void`/`voidLocked`.
