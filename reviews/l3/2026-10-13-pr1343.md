Tier A (egress, localapi, localsrv, localui, cmd). Reviewed from the diff, #1265, ONB-3, CRED-1, CRED-5 and egress K23.

- **Correctness.**
  - `custody.setupKey` validates under `mu` (`setupKeyAdapter`), then checks the key without the lock, within 8 s.
  - It takes `mu` again, re-checks `setupOpen`, and stores through `putKeyLocked`, which re-checks the kind clash.
  - A lock or seal during the check stores nothing (`TestSetupKeyCheckHoldsNoLock`). A malformed key is never sent to the provider.
  - Only 2xx stores. 401 and 403 map to refused. Everything else, including 3xx (no redirect is followed on a bare `http.Transport`), 404, 429, 5xx and dial errors, maps to unchecked.
  - The body is drained to at most 64 KiB, and the per-check transport's idle connections are closed.
- **Waits nest.** The chain is 8 s check < 10 s `SetupAPIKeyTimeout` < 15 s `APIKeyTimeout`, pinned by `TestSetupKeyWaitsNest`. Nothing on the path cuts it shorter:
  - the verify socket's http.Server has no WriteTimeout;
  - the sockets server has no per-op deadline;
  - localui's WriteTimeout is 120 s.
- **Mapping.** The codes run end to end and are tested at each hop:
  - vault 403/502 → `modelroute.ErrKeyRefused`/`ErrKeyUnchecked`;
  - agentosd `localsrv.KeyRefused`/`KeyUnchecked`;
  - localsrv codes `key refused`/`key unchecked`;
  - localui typed errors, then `keyErrText`.
- **CRED-1.** The tests check that no error and no page carries the key.
- **localapi.** Two new codes and one new constant. No op was added, and the op-count tests are unchanged.
- **Tests.** The negative paths (401, 403, 429, 500, 503, 302, 404, dial error, no declaration, bad declaration, malformed key, lock during the check) are covered. All pass with `-race`.
- **L1 (later).** No test drives a provider that hangs until the deadline. The timeout is asserted only as a deadline bound on the request context. Proposal: make the wait a custody field (default `SetupKeyCheckWait`), and add a test with a RoundTripper that blocks on `ctx.Done()` under a 50 ms wait and expects 502 with nothing stored.
- **L2 (later).** `AgentosdSetup.ConnectAPIKey` (localui/setuphooks.go:448) allocates and ranges over a map literal on every call. The order is random, and it differs from the sibling hooks' `if refused(err, ...)` style. A switch, or a package-level slice of pairs, would be clearer and deterministic. The behaviour is correct as written, because the codes are distinct.
- **No finding.** `privateOK` still runs before the key check, so a refused key leaves the private-data answer written. This is unchanged by the PR and harmless, because the marker is read only for a provider whose key is held.
