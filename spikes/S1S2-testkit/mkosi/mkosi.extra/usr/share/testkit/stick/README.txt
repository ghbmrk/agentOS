AgentOS test kit drive (spikes S1 and S2)

results/   one text file per PC tested, plus SUMMARY.csv. Safe to share: no serial
           numbers, network addresses, modem IDs, or phone numbers are written there.
private/   call recordings from the S2 modem test. Do not share.
s2.conf    modem test settings. Erase your number when testing is done.

Don't share this drive or a copy of it: the modem test's logs stay in memory
only, but the drive holds your settings and recordings.

If Windows offers to format a drive or partition, click Cancel. The other
partitions on this drive are Linux and are supposed to look unreadable.
