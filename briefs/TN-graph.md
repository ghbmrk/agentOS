# TN-graph: the owner's graph engine, cut sets, cut-off sets and the scoreboard

Board row: issue #2857 (D-149 §7 package 7; scope comments 6372550831 (D-151) and 6373242443 (D-152)). Tier A (`broker/owner/`, by `tools/risk_tier.py`'s `owner` entry). Release. Size M; estimate 120k tokens, checkpoint at 90k.

## Problem

D-149 makes one graph of the owner's accounts, credentials, providers, devices, keys and people the source of every list the box produces: thief cut sets, cut-off sets, rotation lists, unset brakes, correlated safeguards. KRN-11 says the cut sets, cut-off sets and roots at home are measured by the graph and shown by the scoreboard (CH-12), and CLK-10 says the scoreboard shows how much rests on unverified answers. Nothing on main computes any of it. This package builds the engine against fixture households, before declarations carry real graph facts. It is advisory: it holds no key, sends nothing, and enforcement stays in the clerks and the kernel (D-149 "One loop").

Gate: the row says "after A21". The engine is pure computation over fixtures and wires nothing, so the build does not depend on A21's outcome; the coordinator decides when to start it.

## Design

New package `broker/owner/graph`, standard library only.

- **Nodes.** `Node{ID, Kind, Name, Domains, Assets, Page, Set, Owner, LastUse, Source}`. `Kind` is one of account, credential, provider, device, key, person, node, surface (HW-17, CLK-14), power, uplink (D-152) and brake. `Domains` are failure-domain labels (`maker:yubico`, `phone:ana`, `provider:google`). Two hardware keys from one maker carry the same `maker:` label, so they are one failure domain: the engine counts domains, never nodes. `Assets` are the classes a node carries: money, identity, data, safety. `Page` is the provider page that sets a brake or applies a fix; `Set` is a brake's state; `Owner` marks the owner's person node.
- **Edges.** `Edge{Kind, From []string, To, Source}`. Kinds: reset (can reset), copy (holds a copy of), act (can act on), cutoff (its loss cuts off `To`), brake (`From[0]` brakes `To`), watch (`From[0]` is a witness that sees `To`). An edge with several `From` is an AND: all of them are needed. Several edges into one `To` are an OR: any one is enough. cutoff, brake and watch edges have exactly one `From`; `New` refuses an edge that breaks this or names an unknown node.
- **Source and confidence.** Every node and edge carries its source: shipped (the shipped table), answer (the owner's answer) or observed (a clerk's own observation) (CLK-10). `Confidence(class)` counts the edges by source over every edge that can reach an asset of that class (the edge's `To` is that asset or one of its ancestors over reset, copy and act edges). The scoreboard shows the answer share.
- **What a thief takes.** For a set D of failure domains, a node is compromised when one of its domains is in D, or when some reset, copy or act edge into it has every `From` compromised; iterate to a fixed point. An asset node of class money, identity or safety is *taken* under D when it is compromised and not bounded. It is bounded when it has a brake edge from a brake node that is `Set` and that brake (or the asset itself) has a watch edge from a node not compromised under D: the effect passes a delay a witness in another failure domain sees (KRN-11). A data asset is taken whenever it is compromised, since a read cannot be delayed (KRN-11 Data). Safety assets follow the edges the fixture gives; where a home has no enforcer, the fixture's edges let the core alone reach them (KRN-11).
- **Cut sets.** `CutSets(class)` returns, per asset of that class, every failure domain that alone takes it (size 1) and every pair that takes it where neither member does alone (size 2, minimal), sorted. The domains enumerated are the union of all nodes' labels.
- **Cut-off sets.** The owner reaches a node from the owner's person nodes over reset, copy and act edges (AND and OR as above). For each provider (kinds provider, power, uplink), remove it and every node a cutoff edge from it names, and recompute reach; `CutOffs(class)` lists, per asset, each provider whose removal leaves that asset unreached. An asset the owner does not reach even with nothing removed is listed as unreached, not as cut off.
- **`STOP <name>` reach and rotation (CH-11, D-151).** `Resolve(name)` matches a node's `Name` or `ID`, ignoring case; no match returns false, which the caller reads as "pause everything". `Reach(id)` is the forward closure over reset, copy and act edges in which an edge fires when any of its `From` is in the set: wider than the thief's closure, on purpose, since an over-wide pause costs only a pause. `Rotation(id)` lists the accounts that recover through the node (the closure over reset edges alone) and what it holds a copy of, sorted.
- **Proposals.** `Proposal{Text, Minutes, Structural, AddNodes, AddEdges, DropEdges, SetBrakes}` is a candidate change: a connection, a standing key or a fix. `Apply` returns a new graph and never changes the old one. `Board()` gives, per class, `ThiefNeed` (the smallest cut set's size across its assets: 1, 2, or 3 meaning "more than two") and `CutOff` (the number of distinct providers in its cut-off sets). `Effect(p)` returns the boards before and after (CH-12: every proposal shows its effect before the owner approves it). Its gain is the sum over classes of the rise in `ThiefNeed` and the fall in `CutOff`.
- **Scoreboard and next action.** `Weakest()` is the class with the lowest `ThiefNeed`, then the highest `CutOff`, then the order money, identity, safety, data. `Next(candidates)` picks, among candidates with gain above zero, the structural ones first, then the highest gain per owner minute (`Minutes` of at least 1), then by `Text`; none qualifies, none is returned. `Line(next)` is STATUS's first line: the weakest class, how many failures take it, and the next action's text, or that nothing is needed. It is GSM-7 and at most 160 characters (CH-12), and names no internal part.
- **STATUS lists (A22 Graph, CLK-11).** `Lines(candidates)` returns, in this order: each money, identity or safety asset that one failure domain takes, naming the asset and the domain and, where a candidate removes that cut, the fix's text; each brake on a money or identity asset that is not `Set`, with its page; each AND edge two of whose `From` share a failure domain (a pair of safeguards that fails together); each provider that can cut the owner off from an asset. Every line says what the owner can do (a fix, a page) or names the asset at stake; no line names an ID or a domain label's prefix as written in code.
- **Idle accounts (D-152).** `Idle(now, threshold)` lists accounts whose `LastUse` is set and older than the threshold (default `DefaultIdle`, 12 months), sorted by `LastUse`. Asking the owner about them, paced (CH-15), and closing through the declaration's exit (CLK-14) belong to later rows.
- **STATUS count (D-152).** `Count()` gives the number of accounts and devices; `CountLine(start Counts)` reads `12 accounts, 4 devices (+2 this month)`, with the change against the month's starting count the caller keeps.
- **Downtime (D-152).** `Downtime(entries []time.Time, failed []Span, from, to time.Time, gap time.Duration)` is the length of the union of every gap between consecutive log entries longer than `gap` and every failed-connection span, clipped to `[from, to)`. A window with no entries is all downtime.
- **Fixtures.** `fixture_test.go` builds households in Go, at least: *Ana*, two hardware keys of one maker, a phone, a mailbox that resets the bank and the carrier, a bank with a set new-payee brake watched from the mailbox and an unset card-limit brake, a registrar, a car, a lock behind an enforcer, power and an uplink with cutoff edges; and *Ben*, the same with no enforcer and one key. Each fixture states its expected cut sets, cut-off sets and board in the test, worked out by hand.
- **Advisory only.** The package imports only the standard library and nothing imports it in this package's PR; wiring STATUS, `STOP <name>` and proposals to it is later rows' work.

## Requirements (local IDs; SPEC KRN-11, CLK-10, CH-12)

- **TN-graph-a.** Nodes, AND and OR edges and failure domains as above; `New` refuses a malformed edge; two keys of one maker are one failure domain (KRN-11, CLK-10).
- **TN-graph-b.** Size-1 and minimal size-2 cut sets per asset for money, identity, data and safety, with the brake and witness bound applied to all but data (KRN-11).
- **TN-graph-c.** Cut-off sets per asset, power and uplink counted as providers, unreached assets reported apart (KRN-11, D-152).
- **TN-graph-d.** `STOP <name>` resolution, reach (an unresolved name returns false) and rotation (CH-11, D-151).
- **TN-graph-e.** A proposal's effect on the board, without changing the graph it was applied to; the weakest class and the next action, structural first, then gain per minute (CH-12).
- **TN-graph-f.** Confidence counts edges by source per class (CLK-10).
- **TN-graph-g.** STATUS lines: single-domain takes with their fix, unset brakes with their page, shared-domain safeguard pairs, cut-off providers; `Line` is GSM-7 and at most 160 characters (CH-12, CLK-11, A22 Graph).
- **TN-graph-h.** Idle accounts, the STATUS count line and downtime (D-152).
- **TN-graph-i.** The package imports only the standard library (advisory, D-149).

## Acceptance tests

**Acceptance tests:** all in `broker/owner/graph/graph_test.go` (new). TN-graph-a `TestTwoKeysOfOneMakerAreOneDomain` (catches counting nodes instead of domains), `TestNewRefusesMalformedEdges` (catches an AND brake edge or an unknown node); TN-graph-b `TestFixtureCutSets` (Ana and Ben match their hand-worked cut sets; catches a missed AND, a non-minimal pair, or a brake bounding data), `TestCutSetsMatchBruteForce` (seeded random graphs of up to 8 domains, each reported cut checked against exhaustive enumeration of every domain set of size 1 and 2; catches a missed or extra cut); TN-graph-c `TestFixtureCutOffs` (catches power or the uplink left out, or an unreached asset reported as cut off); TN-graph-d `TestStopReachAndRotation` (catches an unresolved name read as an empty reach, or an AND edge left out of the pause); TN-graph-e `TestProposalEffectLeavesGraphUnchanged`, `TestNextActionStructuralFirst` (catches a higher-gain non-structural action chosen over a structural one, or a zero-gain one chosen); TN-graph-f `TestConfidenceBySource`; TN-graph-g `TestStatusLines` (each list against Ana's; every line ASCII-printable, names no ID; `Line` at most 160 characters, GSM-7); TN-graph-h `TestIdleAccounts`, `TestCountLine`, `TestDowntime` (catches overlapping gap and failure spans counted twice, or a span outside the window counted); TN-graph-i `TestImportsOnlyStdlib` (parses the package's non-test files).

## Scope

`broker/owner/graph/graph.go`, `cut.go`, `board.go`, `status.go` (new), `broker/owner/graph/graph_test.go`, `fixture_test.go` (new), `broker/owner/graph/ASSUMPTIONS.md` (new), this brief. **Recurring kinds (UX lens):** a STATUS line gives its cause and next step, and owner texts never name internal parts (CH-12); `TestStatusLines` checks both.
