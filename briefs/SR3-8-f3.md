**What happens on main (a789f03).** SR3-8-f2 routes every fsync through `fileSync`/`dirSync` (`broker/cleanroom/intake.go:321-336`), which call the hook and then `syncFile`/`syncDirFn`. The builder and intake call sites pass a nil hook (`faultFn(nil)`), and no recorder test covers them. `TestSyncsOnlyThroughSeam` pins *who* calls the seam, not *that* the call happens, so deleting the whole call leaves no trace.
