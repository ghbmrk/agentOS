**Scope:** `broker/cmd/agentos-egress/enroll.go` and `server.go` (enroll handler only), `broker/modelroute/verify.go` (enroll errors only), `broker/cmd/agentosd/setup.go`, `broker/localsrv/setup.go`, `broker/localapi/setup.go`, `broker/localui/setup.go`, `setuphooks.go`, `pages.go`, their tests and ASSUMPTIONS.md (egress K17, localui L28). ## Findings

1. **Never opened reads as sealed.** The vault answers 410 both when setup sealed the seed and when `init -setup` never opened enrollment (`enrolledLocked`). agentosd relays both as `enrolled`; the page shows "Approval codes are already set up… Continue", and finish then refuses (`not enrolled`) with "Could not finish setup. Try again.", a step offered that cannot work.
2. **Lost record after a successful seal.** Finish's seal succeeds, then `Record.Save` fails or agentosd dies: record `Enrolled=true, Finished=false`. A plain retry finishes (the seal answers closed, accepted). If the owner resets setup first, the codes step's enroll clears `Enrolled` before the vault answers 410, and every later finish is refused.

## Change

- **Vault (K17):** enrollment's closed state has two answers. *Sealed by setup* (an `owner-totp-enrolled` entry of its kind; only `/enroll/seal` writes it) stays 410. *Not open* (no open entry, or an entry of the wrong kind) answers 412. `modelroute` maps 412 to `ErrEnrollNotOpen`.
- **agentosd (`localsrv.Setup`):** a new refusal `enrollment unavailable` (`localapi.ErrEnrollUnavailable`) means this box cannot finish setup. Enroll and confirm answer it for a not-open vault, and for a sealed vault when the record holds no confirmation; a sealed vault with a confirmation recorded answers `enrolled` (finish will accept it). Finish accepts *sealed* only with `Enrolled` recorded, as today, and answers `enrollment unavailable` for *not open*.
- Enroll clears `Enrolled` only **after** the vault hands out a new seed, so a refused enroll keeps the evidence of the earlier confirmation. The vault stays the gate: a crash between the new seed and the clear leaves a pending seed, which the seal refuses.
- **Page:** `ErrCodesUnavailable`; the codes step says the box cannot finish setup and offers no Continue. Finish's refusal maps to the same wording, not "Try again".

**Design choice (recorded, not guessed past):** finish does **not** accept a sealed vault when the record holds no confirmation (record deleted with no owner state). That case cannot be told apart from tampering with the drive, and the vault seal is what keeps a deleted record from reopening setup with a new owner number. It stays refused, with the cannot-finish wording.

## Acceptance tests

1. Egress: a vault never opened answers 412 to enroll, confirm and seal; a sealed one 410; a wrong-kind seal entry 412.
2. localsrv: on a never-opened vault, enroll and finish answer `enrollment unavailable`.
3. localsrv: seal succeeds, record save fails; enroll (reset) answers `enrolled`; finish then succeeds with the owner's number.
4. localsrv: sealed vault, empty record: enroll answers `enrollment unavailable` and finish is refused.
5. localui: on `enrollment unavailable` the codes page says the box cannot finish setup, has no Continue and no "already set up".
