# P2-3w: Modem bridge

Board section: Phase 2: real hardware (cloud parts).

Modem bridge: `agentos-modem` drives the modems as its own user and carries texts between them and agentosd's `owner.sock` (`bridgeproto`: inbound, outbox, sent, state), with agentosd's end a `modem.Modem` (`modemlink`) so the owner channel's own texts go out (CH-1, CH-2, ADP-12, ARC-2) ([design](../broker/modem/bridge/DESIGN.md); [assumptions](../broker/modem/bridge/ASSUMPTIONS.md)). Lens conditions: security S-B1–S-B8, UX U-B1–U-B4, potency C1 (part 4 ships with part 2), R1 (late delivery op), R2 (persisted second-SIM budget). Parts: 1 owner channel texts; 2 second-line texts with 4 delivery status; 3 calls (own strong-tier design review). Carry, before the line goes live: the local UI's home page shows `Link.OwnerLineNote` and the last outage with its counts by kind (`Link.LastOutage`) and the bridge's counts (`Others`, `TimedOut`, `Dropped`), with the note saying "below" in place of "on my Wi-Fi page" (U-B1, UX-170-1, UX nit on #170 for P2-2w); before real hardware (S1/S2), the channel re-texts still-valid open requests on recovery, each with a fresh ID (U-B2, B4; UX and potency R1 on #170); part 2: an owner item goes ahead of queued second-line items (potency R2 on #170) and STOP/STATUS replies go ahead of the queue (security R2 on #170); an exact STOP from the owner's number skips the inbound rate limit and is handled first (security R1 at bc36b57); P2-1 installs `/etc/agentos/modem.env` root:root 0600 and creates `/var/lib/agentos/modem` (the unit binds it at start) (L3 on #170)
