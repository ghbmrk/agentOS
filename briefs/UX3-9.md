## Acceptance evidence

1. With two held effects, one uncertain effect and one cancellation-supported effect, STOP immediately blocks new dispatch with all models down. Receipt work cannot delay the stop.
2. Before sign-in show only a safe generic explanation that sent work may still finish and STOP does not undo it; after sign-in show authorized counts/identities and real undo deadlines.
3. Distinguish cancellation requested from confirmed cancelled, and irreversible completed effects from undone. Reload, restart and transport failure cannot turn an unknown state into success.
4. RESUME preserves current authentication and regenerated undo windows; show what held work may restart. Run the existing STOP latency, journal and RESUME security regressions on Linux.
