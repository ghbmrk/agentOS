## Acceptance

1. Rehearse the assembled production path on qualified hardware first. A prototype study before c4 is labelled exploratory and cannot close A1. Freeze revisions, hardware, accounts, workload, numeric targets, repeats and rollback triggers before qualification.
2. Run the required ≥3 PC vendors including floor, with novice participants. A first diagnostic round of eight across current iOS/Android and authenticator experience is a proposal, not a replacement for A1 or a reliability estimate. Repeat confirmation with fresh novices after fixes.
3. Use the unopened kit and a simple instruction to set up and finish useful work. Include qualified local-only, plan-only and API/hosted paths; use synthetic accounts/content. Do not require a mailbox, paid route, backup or extra account for the minimum path.
4. Time power-on, SSID, join, page, pairing, authenticator, recovery, trust, qualified route, All set, first task admitted, result and owner acceptance separately. Record active minutes versus waits, typing, wrong-source codes, switches, re-entry, assistance and abandonment; compare against baseline without claiming invented targets.
5. After the unassisted baseline, test one failure/return per participant: join/page failure, delayed SMS, app-context loss, rejected password/key, opt-out save failure, interruption, key replacement or provider expiry/denial/exhaustion. Test available recovery, not an unsupported reset workaround.
6. After actions, ask without prompting what trust/private-data/fallback cost/recovery choices mean. Distinguish comprehension from checked boxes. Verify STOP/HELP with models down under A4 and record first task accepted with a pre-agreed useful-result rubric.
7. Ready receipt names the saved trust choice, available qualified task classes, owner contact and three suitable examples. First-task help is optional after required setup; route connected is not qualification, submitted credentials are not durable success, and no unsupported class is advertised.
8. Keep authority, recovery, update, privacy and route qualification invariants simultaneously passing. Local-only accepts listed classes and declines others with the reason. Plan exhaustion does not silently add a paid provider; preserve capability discovery, learning and parallel work.
9. Record only synthetic/redacted content and local event timing; no real seeds, codes, card values, account contents or message bodies. Publish failed branches and per-step ranges with denominators; a small sample is diagnostic, not a population completion-rate claim.

## Potency, security and delivery

Measures owner effort and actual capability together. Field testing costs hardware/user time; it does not add product friction. This acceptance-plan work is tier C; any implementation follow-up gets its own risk tier and security gates.

Implementation must be claimed in the owning lane before coding. Intake does not close this item. Record progress at the checkpoint rather than treating it as a spending ceiling.
