## Finding and evidence

[Review finding F2](../reviews/security/2026-10-08-architecture-review.md#f2) at main `7b753eb87f606ea2268ca536d429d47989dba196`.

**Confidence:** Reproduced with unchanged grants implementation, real journal, synthetic clock and executor.
**Exposure:** The grants gate is wired. The demonstrated external operation is a synthetic adapter; current main does not wire the mail adapter. This is a condition to fix before relying on pre-allowed external effects.

The rule checks AuthorizedSince over the preceding 24 hours. An authorized action can remain queued under STOP until its authorization ages out. Dispatch rechecks the same authorization-time history; executing the old action does not add a current entry to that history.

**Observed:** A rule allowing one action per day and one per record per day admitted one queued action on each synthetic day. After advancing time, all three dispatched at the same instant, for the same record, with zero additional approval requests.

**Impact:** An accumulated queue can exceed the owner's execution-rate limits after RESUME. The same query pattern is also used by evidence-delivery and mail-organizing limits; those paths need a focused follow-up, not an assumption that this proof covers them.

## Scope and intended change

`broker/grants/` and `broker/journal/`, including tests and ASSUMPTIONS.md. Inspect other consumers of AuthorizedSince; split any unrelated fix into a separately claimed package.

Separate pending reservations from actual dispatch accounting. At dispatch, atomically reserve the current window and retain in-flight/unknown outcomes until reconciled. Release a reservation only on a defined non-effect outcome. Alternatively expire and reauthorize old queued actions while preserving STOP and approval semantics.

Do not expand authority or edit SPEC.md in this package. If the cited contract cannot decide a design choice, raise an L1 spec-diff proposal before implementation. Record changed assumptions in the affected package's ASSUMPTIONS.md.

## Acceptance tests

1. Configure one action per day and one per record per day. Under STOP, queue authorized operations on separate synthetic days; advance the clock and RESUME. Dispatch must obey the current window for the service and record, holding or asking for the remainder.
2. Race two dispatches against the final available allowance. Assert at most one external effect starts; restart between durable reservation and execution and preserve the reservation.
3. Retain accounting for in-flight/unknown outcomes until reconciliation. Define and test cancellation, confirmed non-effect failure, retry, and window-boundary behavior without double charging or releasing uncertain capacity.
4. Inspect each evidence-delivery/mail-organizing caller of AuthorizedSince and record whether it has the same dispatch defect. Add a focused regression or a linked release item for each affected caller; do not claim the synthetic adapter proves them all.
