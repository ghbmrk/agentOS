# 2573-f6: an approval is the owner's passkey link (#2675)

Board row: #2675 (class:release, tier A, lane:primary). Source: the round-1 reviews of PR #2673, and the #2674 combined later item 6322318841 (added on #2675 as 6322330182). It builds on #2673 and #2680, and on the kernel's owner-signed narrowing link and `clerk.Verify` (PR #2686, KRN-ownerlink). Usage estimate: 60k tokens.

**Why (facts).** After #2673, the browser clerk takes a key as the owner's approval of exact submits when its content set holds approval prints only and its uses are capped at their number. The home clerk can derive such a key itself, so a compromised home clerk could approve its own submit. And `approval()` checked the chain against the hardware anchors only, so a key the phone's passkey signed was refused.

## Requirements (from SPEC.md)
- **CLK-3, unknown submits:** a submit is released only on the owner's approval of the exact request.
- **OWN-9:** the owner approves by passkey.
- **KRN-8:** a clerk performs a step at most once.

## Design
The tap is a link that the owner's passkey signs over the home clerk's key, for the same holder (`key.Narrow`). Its caveats are the prints, a use cap and an expiry within `MaxApproval` (15 minutes), so one tap approves one task's submits, not every submit until the clerk key lapses (#2686 combined 6323127608). `Clerk.Do` gets the key's chain from `clerk.Verify`, the same check `clerk.Check` makes, which trusts every enrolled passkey and refuses one cut off since. A post needs `Verified.ByOwner`, and the tip link itself must carry a content caveat, a use cap no larger than the key's number of prints, and an expiry within `MaxApproval` of now. The key's whole content set must hold approval prints only.

## Package IDs
- **BRS-12 Only the passkey approves.** A key the home clerk derives with the right prints and use cap is refused `unapproved`. So is a passkey link over such a key that names no prints, caps no uses, or expires more than `MaxApproval` after the post. So is a link the home clerk adds below the owner's tap. The same caveats on the passkey's own link send once.
- **BRS-13 Every enrolled passkey counts.** An approval the enrolled phone signs releases a post. The test rig taps every approval with it.
