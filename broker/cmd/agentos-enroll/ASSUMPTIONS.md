# cmd/agentos-enroll assumptions

Built for OWN-card-flow slice a (#3470, briefs/OWN-card-flow.md). Earlier rows for this command live in `broker/clerk/seal/ASSUMPTIONS.md` (STO-2c, MIG-4-recovery-r2). Each row is a reading a reviewer may want to change.

| # | Assumption | Spec basis | If it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| OWN-a-1 | **The staged pair is the only authority enrollment stores at first boot.** `-stage-page-ca` makes it before the Owner Card is shown and writes its sealed form (`/run/agentos-setup/page-ca`, 0600) and then its certificate (`/run/agentos-page-ca/authority.der`, 0644), each by a hidden temporary name, `fsync` and `link`, so neither is ever overwritten or part-written under its final name (`writeOnce`). Rerun: both present and one pair, nothing; the pair alone, publish its certificate; the certificate alone, or two files that are not one pair, or a link at either name, refused with fixed words. With `-page-ca-file`, enrollment reads the pair (`readSecret`, bounded at `pageca.MaxSealed`), runs `pageca.Unmarshal`'s load checks, reads the certificate through its descriptor (no symlink, regular, root's, not group- or world-writable, at most 4 KiB) and requires it byte for byte; only then does anything change, and it stores the bytes as read. A rerun after a crash stores the same pair. Without the flag (tests, a restore's enrollment) it still makes its own. The image's unit always passes the flag. | HW-1, CH-9, STO-2 | If the kernel ever serves an authority not from the store, the card's fingerprint must come from there instead. |
| OWN-a-2 | **`-take-factors` is the only writer of the factor files, and answers only `ok`, `bad`, `authority`, `taken` or `kept`.** One JSON request of at most 8 KiB to the end of stdin, unknown fields refused, `v` 1. `bad`: it does not read, the passphrase is not the card's shape (seven words of a to z and inner hyphens, 3 to 9 long, single spaces: `card.NormalizePassphrase`'s form, which the store seals as is), or the key is not 40 card symbols whose every group check holds. With a store in place it opens the passphrase slot with the offered passphrase: `ok` if it opens, `kept` if not, writing nothing. Otherwise `authority` unless the published certificate exists and its `pageca.Fingerprint` equals the request's exactly. Files already present are compared in constant time before anything is written: this card's are kept (`ok`, a retried confirm, or one cut between the two writes, which the retry finishes), another card's in either file is `taken`. Then the passphrase and the recovery key (`card.StoreRecoveryKey` of the text, 64 hex digits, as `factors()` reads it) are written once, recovery last. An I/O failure answers no word and leaves a fixed reason in the journal: the page reads that as a keeper it could not reach. No factor or part of the request reaches the answer or the journal (`TestTakeFactorsNeverLogsAFactor`). The factors are read into byte slices, never strings, and wiped on return. | HW-1, STO-2 | If the page needs to tell an I/O failure from an unreachable keeper, add a sixth word. |
| OWN-a-3 | **The keeper reads the card's formats itself, not by linking `broker/card`.** The brief's decision 1 says `agentos-enroll` already links `card`'s key derivation; it does not, and `card` links its page, its QR encoder and, through them, `owner`, `control` and `modem`, each a parser in `tools/trusted_base_parsers.txt`, into this root binary. So `take.go` reads the recovery key and the passphrase's shape from the format alone, as `agentos-a8scan` does (#3500), and `TestCardFormatMatchesCard` holds both equal to `card`'s over 200 random keys in four typed forms, a mistyped symbol in every group position, every passphrase `card.Generate` makes and every word on its list. `card` is linked only by the tests. No `card.ValidPassphrase` is added. | HW-1, KRN-2 | If `card`'s key or passphrase format changes, the test fails until `take.go` follows. |
