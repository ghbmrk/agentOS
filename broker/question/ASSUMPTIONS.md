# Questions with defaults: assumptions

Built for P3-8 against SPEC v0.12 (CAP-10, REV-2, REV-5, CH-15, TIM-1).
Each row is a reading of the spec, or a gap left for a later package,
that a reviewer may want to change.

| # | Assumption | Spec basis | If it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | A guest asks through two broker tools, `owner_question` and `owner_question_status` (`Tools`, `Book.Call`). The asker is the guest's lineage (guest G4) and questions are keyed by its own `request_id`, so a retry or a fork repeating its source's call is the same question, texted once; the same `request_id` with a different question is refused. Another lineage cannot read a question. | CAP-10, OP-1 | — |
| Q2 | The question carries a default and a wait. The broker bounds both: the default is required, one line, at most 80 characters, and one of the choices when choices are given (at most 4, each at most 40); the question is one line of at most 200 characters; the wait is required and clamped to 5 min .. 24 h. Control, format and line-separator characters become spaces, so a question cannot lay out what looks like a broker template. The question, default and choices may not hold a 6 to 8 digit number (folded and joined as in Q5) or a channel reply with an ID or code after it (`YES A4`, `RESUME 482913`), so a question never hands the owner a reply to copy. Nor may they name a credential (security C2 on #71): code, OTP, PIN, password, passphrase, recovery, seed, grid, cell, card, 2FA, verification, authenticator, and plurals, also with punctuation inside the word dropped ("pass-word", "P.I.N."). The agent never needs one, so only a phishing question would ask. The list is a backstop, not the boundary: Q8 (no authority) and Q5's answer filters are. A choice may not start, after the channel's own word split (anything but letters and digits is a space), with a channel word the owner could send alone (STOP, STATUS, HELP, YES, NO, UNDO, MORE, PUBLIC, RUN, RESUME, UNLOCK, PAUSE, REVOKE), since the channel takes it. A default may, because the owner answers with the tag ("Q104 no"), which the channel never takes. The rendered text, with a worst-case deadline, must fit `MaxRendered` (452 bytes: `control.MaxText` less `owner.AgentPrefix`, pinned by `owner/question_test.go`), so `Fit` never cuts the default or deadline. A question `Config.Hidden` (`owner.SecretShaped`) would withhold is refused at Ask, since the owner would see only a pointer while it defaulted. Retries compare the question after these bounds (a 1 s and a 2 s wait are the same question). | CAP-10, CH-12, CH-19 | `Default*`/`Max*` constants. |
| Q3 | At most 3 open questions per lineage and 8 on the box. Texts are paced at 3 an hour (CH-15's default for unsolicited texts), persisted so a restart does not reset the hour, and held in quiet hours; a held question has no deadline yet. Oldest first, but a lineage texted in the last hour waits behind one that was not. **The deadline starts when the owner is texted**, not when the guest asks, so pacing, quiet hours or a modem outage never turn into a silent default. | CAP-10, CH-15 | **Binding on W9:** questions and approval requests draw on one CH-15 budget (the wiring passes the channel's counter). |
| Q4 | The text is `Q104: <question> Choices: 1) .. Reply Q104 and your answer by 14:30. No reply: it goes ahead with "<default>".` It goes out through `owner.Channel.Notify`, so the whole text is behind the agent prefix and secret-shaped content is withheld (CH-19): the question is agent-written. | CAP-10, CH-19 | — |
| Q5 | A reply is matched by its leading tag (`Q104 yes`, `q104: yes`). Tags are Q100-Q999: four characters, so never an approval request ID (a letter and one or two digits); `YES Q104` is not an approval reply and `Q104 yes` is not a channel word (`owner/question_test.go`). A tag is not reused while its question is kept (7 days after close); since questions close only after a text, `Keep` is capped below 900 / SendsPerHour hours, so the tag space cannot run out. An untagged text is chat, as today, with one exception (UX R2 on #71): when exactly one question is open and texted, no approval request is open on the channel (`Config.ApprovalsOpen`; nil turns this off), and an untagged reply is exactly one of its choices, it answers that question. Choices are never channel words (Q2), so the channel never takes such a reply first. With choices, the answer must be one of them, by text or number. The first answer is final; a later one is told so. **Answers can't carry a code or key** (security C1 on #71): a 6 to 8 digit number, with Unicode digits counted and groups joined across up to three separators that are not letters (`482 913`, `482/913`, `482:913`, `4, 8, 2, 9, 1, 3`), after blanking shapes that are not codes: valid clock times (`9:30`), dates (`05/10/2026`, `2026-10-05`, `2026/27`), and amounts with a currency sign, a decimal part, or two or more thousands groups (`£1,250.00`, `1,234,567`). `482,913` alone stays code-shaped; anything `Config.Hidden` (`owner.SecretShaped`) withholds; or part of a recovery key: split on anything but letters and digits, two or more five-symbol groups, or one run of ten or more, of card-alphabet symbols that mix letters and digits (words do not mix them). Codes and keys go only to the box, so a question cannot phish one for the guest. Each refusal is counted, and the digest reports the count as a guard hit (security R1). | CAP-10, CH-4 | Allow untagged replies when exactly one question is open (UX). |
| Q6 | The broker's timer decides the lapse (`Tick`), and an answer arriving after the deadline but before the tick counts as late, never as on time (outside the restart grace, Q11). Nothing the guest sends moves a question. A late reply is passed to the guest as `late_owner_reply` and the owner is told "Too late for Q104: the agent went ahead with … Your answer is passed to it." | CAP-10 | — |
| Q7 | Every defaulted question is a digest line, and refused answers are one line with their count (Q5) (`TakeDigest`, persisted until taken): `Q4 "<question>": no reply by 14:30, so the agent went ahead with "<default>".` | CAP-10, CH-12 | — |
| Q8 | **A default carries no authority, and neither does an answer.** An answer is chat, not an approval code. The package imports nothing that can approve (journal, grants, owner, control, vault, egress; `boundary_test.go`), and `grants/question_test.go` proves that a send the guest asked about, with a "yes" default or a "yes" text answer cited in its params, still waits for its own approval request and runs only on that decision. | CAP-10, REV-2 | — |
| Q9 | Time comes from `Config.Now(ctx) (time.Time, error)`, the signature of `clock.Guard.Now` (P2-9, #68). While it errors (restricted), nothing is texted and nothing lapses; `owner_question_status` reports `held` with a clock reason. The owner can still answer, since an answer needs no clock. Until #68 merges the wiring passes a function over the wall clock. | TIM-1 | — |
| Q10 | An owner answer is owner data, so `owner_question_status` raises the reading machine to private (`Config.Reveal`, wired to `vm.Manager.RaisePrivate`) before returning it; a failed raise returns nothing. A default is agent text and raises nothing. | REV-5 | — |
| Q11 | Questions, answers, pending digest lines and the hour's sends persist to `Config.Path` (0600, written through, atomic rename, directory fsynced). A failure to save after an effect that already happened (a text sent, a lapse) is logged, not ignored; Ask and Answer fail instead when they cannot save. After a restart, lapsing waits a 2 min grace from the first trusted tick, so replies the carrier queued while the box was down arrive first. Closed questions are kept 7 days for status, then dropped. | CAP-10, OP-4 | — |

## Wiring (W-row on BOARD, not in this package)

- Guest plane: list `question.Tools` next to `effect_request`, and route the two names to `Book.Call(ctx, lineage, machine, name, args)`.
- Owner channel: call `Book.Answer` for text the channel would pass to the agent, after the unlock check and code stripping (CH-3, CH-14), before delegating; if it returns `ok`, send its reply instead.
- `agentosd`: `Send` = `Channel.Notify`, `Now` = `clock.Guard.Now`, `Reveal` = `vm.Manager.RaisePrivate`, `Quiet` = the owner's quiet hours, `Path` under the broker's state directory, `Hidden` = `owner.SecretShaped`, `ApprovalsOpen` = whether the channel has a request open, `Logf`, `Run` on a 30 s ticker, and `TakeDigest` into the daily digest.
- Pacing: questions and approval requests share one CH-15 budget (Q3), and approval requests go first in it (UX-71-1).
