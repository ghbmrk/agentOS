# Questions with defaults: assumptions

Built for P3-8 against SPEC v0.12 (CAP-10, REV-2, REV-5, CH-15, TIM-1).
Each row is a reading of the spec, or a gap left for a later package,
that a reviewer may want to change.

| # | Assumption | Spec basis | If it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | A guest asks through two broker tools, `owner_question` and `owner_question_status` (`Tools`, `Book.Call`). The asker is the guest's lineage (guest G4) and questions are keyed by its own `request_id`, so a retry or a fork repeating its source's call is the same question, texted once; the same `request_id` with a different question is refused. Another lineage cannot read a question. | CAP-10, OP-1 | — |
| Q2 | The question carries a default and a wait. The broker bounds both: the default is required, one line, at most 80 characters, and one of the choices when choices are given (at most 4, each at most 40); the question is one line of at most 200 characters; the wait is required and clamped to 5 min .. 24 h. Control, format and line-separator characters become spaces, so a question cannot lay out what looks like a broker template. The question, default and choices may not hold a 6 to 8 digit number (folded and joined as in Q5) or a channel reply with an ID or code after it (`YES A4`, `RESUME 482913`), so a question never hands the owner a reply to copy. Nor may they hold a question tag (`Q` and three digits, any case): questions from several lineages share a text (Q12), so a tag in one could steer the owner's answer to another lineage's question (security F1 on #117). Nor may they name a credential (security C2 on #71): code, OTP, PIN, password, passphrase, recovery, seed, grid, cell, card, 2FA, verification, authenticator, and plurals, also with punctuation inside the word dropped ("pass-word", "P.I.N."). The agent never needs one, so only a phishing question would ask. The list is a backstop, not the boundary: Q8 (no authority) and Q5's answer filters are. A choice may not start, after the channel's own word split (anything but letters and digits is a space), with a channel word the owner could send alone (STOP, STATUS, HELP, YES, NO, UNDO, MORE, PUBLIC, RUN, RESUME, UNLOCK, PAUSE, REVOKE), since the channel takes it. A default may, because the owner answers with the tag ("Q104 no"), which the channel never takes. The rendered text, with a worst-case deadline, must fit `MaxRendered` (452 bytes: `control.MaxText` less `owner.AgentPrefix`, pinned by `owner/question_test.go`), so `Fit` never cuts the default or deadline. A question `Config.Hidden` (`owner.SecretShaped`) would withhold is refused at Ask, since the owner would see only a pointer while it defaulted. Retries compare the question after these bounds (a 1 s and a 2 s wait are the same question). | CAP-10, CH-12, CH-19 | `Default*`/`Max*` constants. |
| Q3 | At most 3 open questions per lineage and 8 on the box. Texts are paced at 3 an hour (CH-15's default for unsolicited texts; a text may carry several questions, Q12), persisted so a restart does not reset the hour, and held in quiet hours; a held question has no deadline yet. Oldest first, but a lineage texted in the last hour waits behind one that was not, and a question held `AgedAfter` leads (Q16). **The deadline starts when the owner is texted**, not when the guest asks, so pacing, quiet hours or a modem outage never turn into a silent default. | CAP-10, CH-15 | **Binding on W9:** questions and approval requests draw on one CH-15 budget (the wiring passes the channel's counter). |
| Q4 | The text is `Q104: <question> Choices: 1) .. Reply Q104 and your answer by 14:30. No reply: it goes ahead with "<default>".` It goes out through `owner.Channel.Notify`, so the whole text is behind the agent prefix and secret-shaped content is withheld (CH-19): the question is agent-written. | CAP-10, CH-19 | — |
| Q5 | A reply is matched by its leading tag (`Q104 yes`, `q104: yes`). Tags are Q100-Q999: four characters, so never an approval request ID (a letter and one or two digits); `YES Q104` is not an approval reply and `Q104 yes` is not a channel word (`owner/question_test.go`). A tag is not reused while its question is kept (7 days after close); only a question that was texted (or is open) holds its tag, since only those showed it to the owner (Q13), and a text carries at most `MaxPerText` questions, so `Keep` is capped at (900 − 2 × MaxOpen) / (SendsPerHour × MaxPerText) − 1 hours (S1 on #117: open questions hold tags too, and a question texted in the hour before the window closes inside it) and the tag space cannot run out. A reply that names a second known tag (`Q104 yes Q105 no`) answers neither: the owner is told "Send one answer per question, like Q104 <default>." (UX-117-1), so no words land in the wrong question. An untagged text is chat, as today, with one exception (UX R2 on #71): when exactly one question is open and texted, no approval request is open on the channel (`Config.ApprovalsOpen`; nil turns this off), and an untagged reply is exactly one of its choices, it answers that question. Choices are never channel words (Q2), so the channel never takes such a reply first. With choices, the answer must be one of them, by text or number. The first answer is final; a later one is told so. **Answers can't carry a code or key** (security C1 on #71): a run of 6 or more digits, with Unicode digits counted and groups joined across up to three separators that are not letters (`482 913`, `482/913`, `482:913`, `4, 8, 2, 9, 1, 3`, `482913 000`). A run is excused only when it is phone-shaped (10 or 11 digits, or 10 to 13 after a `+`) or the whole joined run, exactly, is a strict shape: one clock time, or a list of times on the five minutes and strictly ascending (`9:30, 10:00`; `4:28, 9:13` is refused); a date with a four-digit year and real day and month ranges (`05/10/2026`, `2026-10-05`); a season (`2026/27`); or a year range (`1990-2026`). When in doubt it refuses: amounts are not excused (`£1,250.00`, `1,234,567`; the owner writes "1250 pounds"). **The filter catches a code pasted or typed as received, not one the owner deliberately re-encodes** (spelled out, digits interleaved with letters, or padded inside a phone-shaped run). Accepted residuals: a code inside a 10-11 digit run, a code that happens to be a valid date or ascending five-minute times, and re-encodings; Q8 (no authority) bounds them; anything `Config.Hidden` (`owner.SecretShaped`) withholds; or part of a recovery key: split on anything but letters and digits, two or more five-symbol groups, or one run of ten or more, of card-alphabet symbols that mix letters and digits (words do not mix them). Codes and keys go only to the box, so a question cannot phish one for the guest. Each refusal is counted, and the digest reports the count as a guard hit (security R1). | CAP-10, CH-4 | Allow untagged replies when exactly one question is open (UX). |
| Q6 | The broker's timer decides the lapse (`Tick`), and an answer arriving after the deadline but before the tick counts as late, never as on time (outside the restart grace, Q11). Nothing the guest sends moves a question. A late reply is passed to the guest as `late_owner_reply` and the owner is told "Too late for Q104: the agent went ahead with … Your answer is passed to it." | CAP-10 | — |
| Q7 | Every defaulted question is a digest line, and refused answers are one line with their count (Q5) (`TakeDigest`, persisted until taken): `Q4 "<question>": no reply by 14:30, so the agent went ahead with "<default>".` | CAP-10, CH-12 | — |
| Q8 | **A default carries no authority, and neither does an answer.** An answer is chat, not an approval code. The package imports nothing that can approve (journal, grants, owner, control, vault, egress; `boundary_test.go`), and `grants/question_test.go` proves that a send the guest asked about, with a "yes" default or a "yes" text answer cited in its params, still waits for its own approval request and runs only on that decision. | CAP-10, REV-2 | — |
| Q9 | Time comes from `Config.Now(ctx) (time.Time, error)`, the signature of `clock.Guard.Now` (P2-9, #68). While it errors (restricted), the wait and pacing run on the monotonic clock instead (Q15). With quiet hours configured, nothing is texted then. `owner_question_status` reports a question still unsent `held` with a clock reason. The owner can still answer, since an answer needs no clock. Until #68 merges the wiring passes a function over the wall clock. | TIM-1 | — |
| Q10 | An owner answer is owner data, so `owner_question_status` raises the reading machine to private (`Config.Reveal`, wired to `vm.Manager.RaisePrivate`) before returning it; a failed raise returns nothing. A default is agent text and raises nothing. | REV-5 | — |
| Q11 | Questions, answers, pending digest lines and the hour's sends persist to `Config.Path` (0600, written through, atomic rename, directory fsynced). A failure to save after an effect that already happened (a text sent, a lapse) is logged, not ignored; Ask and Answer fail instead when they cannot save. After a restart, lapsing waits a 2 min grace from the first trusted tick, so replies the carrier queued while the box was down arrive first. Closed questions are kept 4 days for status (capped by Q5 at 97 h with defaults), then dropped. | CAP-10, OP-4 | — |
| Q12 | **Shared texts (PQ1, potency on #71).** When a text is due, the first held question by the Q3 order leads it, and further held questions, in the same order, join it while the joined text still fits `MaxRendered` and `Config.Hidden` would not withhold it, up to `MaxPerText` (3). Each keeps its own tag, rendering and deadline (the send time plus its own wait). The CH-15 budget and `Reserve` count texts, not questions. Every asker in a text counts as texted for Q3's fairness; a lineage texted in the last hour may still join a text another lineage leads, since joining costs no budget. Questions are joined with a space: `control.Fit` turns a line feed into `?` (GSM-7 basic set), so UX R1's line break is not available. | CH-15, CAP-10 | `MaxPerText`. |
| Q13 | **Ask-by (PQ2, potency on #71).** `ask_by_minutes` (optional; clamped like the wait) counts from the ask on the monotonic clock, so a clock restriction neither stretches it nor lets a question past it be texted (L3 MUST on #125); `Asked` is dated from that clock at the first trusted tick. After a restart while restricted the reading is gone, so such a question waits unsent for trusted time and is then judged by `Asked`. A question still unsent when it passes closes `not_asked` at the next tick (while restricted, the line says "held past its ask-by time") and is never texted afterwards; the digest says `Not asked: the agent's question "<question>" was held past 13:20 (texts paced or quiet hours), so the agent went ahead without asking.` (UX on #117) with no tag, since the owner never saw one. It holds no tag, so an owner text with that tag is chat. Once texted, the ask-by no longer applies. Since they hold no tag, not-asked questions are bounded apart (S2 on #117): the newest 32 are kept for status, and past 8 pending digest lines the digest only counts the rest. A restricted clock neither starts nor ends an ask-by (TIM-1). | CAP-10, CH-15 | — |
| Q14 | **Waiting status (PQ3, potency on #71).** `owner_question_status` takes `wait_seconds`, clamped to `MaxPoll` (25 s, as the guest plane's own `/owner/next` poll). It returns when the question's state changes (texted, answered, defaulted, not asked), the wait ends, or the guest's request ends; a closed question answers at once. Each wait holds one of the machine's 8 request slots (guest G1), so at most `WaitersPerMachine` (2) wait per machine; past that the call answers at once, as a plain status. The answer's REV-5 raise (Q10) happens as for a plain status. | CAP-10, ARC-6 | `WaitersPerMachine`, `MaxPoll`. |
| Q15 | **The clock check does not stop questions (PQ4, W9a on #95).** A question's wait needs no trusted wall clock: each text records the monotonic clock (`Config.Mono`). While `Now` is restricted, texts still go out, giving the wait instead of a time ("Reply Q104 and your answer within 2 hours."; `waitWords` drops seconds, so never longer than the wait), and Ask refuses a question that would not fit one text either way. Pacing counts the hour's texts on the monotonic clock. `Tick`, or an answer that arrives first, lapses a texted question once `Wait` has passed on that clock. A deadline set from a trusted time dates the lapse; a question texted while restricted has none, so its digest line says "no reply within 30 minutes" and its late reply gives no time. `owner_question_status` reports it `waiting` with a clock reason. The first trusted tick dates such a question from the monotonic clock (`Sent`, `Deadline`); `Texted` marks it texted until then. With quiet hours configured (`Quiet`), nothing is texted while restricted, since they cannot be told; today's wiring has none. Monotonic time does not survive a restart, so a loaded question texted while restricted waits its whole wait again from the restart, never less, and while restricted a loaded send counts for the hour after the restart. No restart grace applies to a question this process texted, because no reply to it could have been queued while the box was down. A default carries no authority (Q8), so lapsing on the monotonic clock grants nothing (security neutral, potency on #95). | TIM-1, CAP-10, CH-15 | If a restricted clock should hold every question again, drop the restricted path in `sendDue` and `tickRestricted`. |
| Q16 | **An aged question goes ahead (PQ5, W9a on #95).** A question held `AgedAfter` (default an hour) since it was asked leads the next text, ahead of the per-lineage order, and its reservation is `Reserve(true)`. The gate then lets it ahead of a waiting approval batch once an hour (grants GR22), so steady approval traffic never holds a question indefinitely; questions that fit join its text as usual (PQ1). Age is the longer of the monotonic reading since the ask (or since loading, after a restart) and, on trusted time, `Asked`, which is now kept for every question. `ask_by` (Q13) still serves guests that set it; this is the floor for those that do not. Each text logs how long its questions waited, to tune `AgedAfter`. | CAP-10, CH-15 | Tune `AgedAfter` from the logged waits. |

## Wiring (W9, `cmd/agentosd/questions.go`)

- Guest plane: `Config.Tools` lists `question.Tools` after the effect tools and routes the two names to `Book.Call(ctx, lineage, machine, name, args)` (`GuestTools`; guest G15). Tools are offered only when the book opened.
- Owner channel: `control.Handler.Answer` calls `Book.Answer` for text the channel would pass to the agent: from the owner's number, in an unlocked session, after settings, with any code stripped (CH-3, CH-14), never for a PUBLIC task; if it returns `ok`, its reply goes instead and the agent never sees the text.
- `agentosd`: `Send` = `Channel.Notify`, `Now` = `clock.Guard.Now` (a guard opened here and `Run`; P2-9), `Reveal` = `vm.Manager.RaiseLabel(machine, Private)`, `Path` = `-questions`, `Hidden` = `owner.SecretShaped`, `ApprovalsOpen` = `Channel.ApprovalsOpen`, `Logf`, `Run` on a 30 s ticker. With no owner channel the book does not open and nothing is offered.
- Pacing (Q3, UX-71-1): `Reserve` = the gate's `Reserve`, which under the gate's lock refuses while an approval batch waits or when approval request texts and reserved questions in the hour have reached `RequestsPerHour`, and otherwise counts the question's text in the same step. The gate counts each of its own request texts before sending it, and a paced flush checks the budget per text and keeps the rest batched, so the hour never exceeds the budget whichever side sends first (L3 M1 on #95). A failed send keeps its reservation (fewer texts, never more). Urgent requests and an owner active in chat still bypass the budget, as before.
- **Liveness (L3 S1 on #95):** with no carrier time, a restriction the guard reads from an unreadable `clock.json` never lifts, so every question holds and none defaults, silently while `Notify` is off. Fail-safe by design (TIM-1). STATUS shows it (W9a, UX R3): `agentosd` adds the guard's `Status().Line` in the box's zone to `daemon.Config.Notes`, shown unless the sources agree (UX-98-1), so STATUS ends "Time check: restricted since HH:MM (saved check unreadable; waiting for phone-network time)."
- **Not yet wired** (carried on BOARD): `Quiet` (no owner quiet-hours setting exists on the box yet; the gate has none either), `TakeDigest` (no daily digest sender yet; lines persist until one takes them), and the guard's owner texts (`clock.Config.Notify`): its wording promises that pre-allowances, request expiry and updates play safe, which holds only once those move onto the guard (clock K7). Until then a restriction holds questions without a text.
