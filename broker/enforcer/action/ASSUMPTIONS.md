# Enforcer asserted actions: assumptions

Each row is a reading of the spec, or a property the code relies on, that a
reviewer may want to change (briefs/ENF.md slice s, #3694). Covers `action/`
and the page's action routes (`page/act.go`, `templates/action.html`).

| # | Assumption | Spec basis | If it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENF-s-1 | **The acts are a closed list over the safety classes**: turn on (heater, primary heating, oven, plug, relay, camera, microphone), open (valve, feeding valve, garage door), fly one named route (drone), unlock (lock), arm and disarm (alarm), turn off (camera, microphone). A siren, a sensor, a device of unknown type and every everyday device take none here; an isolated device takes none until the owner lifts the isolation. Turning a camera or microphone back on is a turn-on, so it needs the challenge too. | NET-6 ("every turn-on or open of a safety-list device", "every unlock or disarm"), NET-4 Tightening (camera off, arming an alarm) | A new act or class is one table row in `action.go` and one test row. |
| ENF-s-2 | **The window is 2 minutes** (`action.Window`) and the book holds at most 16 live challenges. NET-6 says "a short time window" with no number. A challenge is spent on its first redeem, whether or not the tap verifies, as the page's own challenges are (ENF-b-4). | NET-6 | Slice q may want a different window per class; the window is in the signed message, so it can vary per challenge. |
| ENF-s-3 | **What the passkey signs** is `Domain ‖ device ‖ class ‖ act ‖ route ‖ challenge ID ‖ expiry`, with device and route limited to printable ASCII without spaces, 64 bytes at most, so no field can forge another. The redeem re-reads the policy in force under the page's lock and refuses if the device is gone, isolated, off the safety list or of another class. | NET-6 ("names the lock, the action and a short time window and works once"), NET-4 Passkey rules | — |
| ENF-s-4 | **Asserted actions are not policy versions.** They change no document, so they are not KEY-1 roots and are not in the policy export; the owner's assertion is checked with `key.PublicKey.Verify` like the page's cancel and resume. The asserted tightenings that do change the document (cut the vendor cloud, isolate, remove) stay policy versions applied through `policy.Engine.Propose`, at once (ENF-a-3); `TighteningLink` is their link. | STO-4 (policy is keys), NET-4 | If a log of asserted actions is wanted on the enforcer, slice l writes one from the page's results. |
| ENF-s-5 | **The channel's requests become links, built here and texted by the daemon.** The channel already refuses these (it carries a safety-list device's one secure direction only, and answers a safety-list tightening with `NeedsOwner`). `action.Link` and `action.TighteningLink` build the page link for each; sending it over the enforcer's alert path is slice l's wiring. The core may also text the same link itself: opening it does nothing, the screen is the enforcer's and the tap is the owner's. | NET-4 Tightening ("needs an owner passkey assertion that the enforcer verifies on a signed link") | — |
| ENF-s-6 | **The actuator is an interface** (`action.Actuator`), not `radio.Host` directly: the radio host declares no turn-on, open, arm or fly op yet (slices q and t add their stacks' ops), and a camera is reached over its settings channel, not a radio. The daemon (slice l) adapts each act to the radio host or driver. A drone route is passed by name; checking it against the owner-signed drone policy is DRONE-enforcer's (#3517). | NET-2 Confined radio stacks, brief decision 6 | — |
| ENF-s-7 | **Home only is the page's three checks** (ENF-b-6): the action routes are served by the same handler, so a request from outside the Owner network and the admin port is dropped before any route runs. `action::TestServedOnlyAtHome` holds that for `/a` and `/do`. | NET-6 | — |
| ENF-s-8 | **One actuator call is bounded at `action.Timeout` (10 s, the radio host's own bound)**, and the page's `Config.ActTimeout` may only tighten it. `Book.Redeem` stops waiting then and returns `ErrTimeout`, so a hung actuator cannot hold the policy lock the channel's relocks share; a panicking actuator is a failed act. An act already sent stays ordered before every later command to the same device, because the radio host sends a stack's commands in order on one link, so a relock after a timeout still wins. The tap is reported as "may still do it; check the device". | NET-6, NET-2 Channel | If slice l adapts an act to anything other than the radio host, the adapter must keep that ordering. |
| ENF-s-9 | **The screen names the window as a length** ("within 2 minutes of opening this page"), never a clock time, so the owner converts no time zone; and every refused tap says what to do next. | NET-4 (the owner understands what the page asks) | Other page times shown in UTC are their own row (see the PR's findings). |
