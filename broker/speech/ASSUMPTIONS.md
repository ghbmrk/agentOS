# Speech process: assumptions

Built for VOICE-ro-b part b1 (briefs/VOICE-ro.md, #5171) against SPEC CH-5, CH-17 and CH-21 and the flip-phone note §2.2 to §2.4. Covers `broker/speech`, `broker/speech/wire` and `broker/cmd/agentos-speech`.

| # | Assumption | Basis | If it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| VOICE-ro-b-1 | **The process is untrusted, and so is all it sends.** A model runs in it, so a bad frame (unknown type, oversize, text not UTF-8 or with a control, format or line-separator character (U+2028, U+2029), odd PCM, a reply to a sentence never asked, an end of speech past the audio sent) ends the session; the loop then plays its failed clip (VOICE-ro-a-C8). Nothing is repaired, so a recognizer that emits a stray control character fails calls loudly rather than slipping it to the answerer. | Brief rule 1 for b; flip-phone note §2.2 rule 3 | If the chosen recognizer (b2) emits such characters in normal use, strip them in the process before they reach the wire, not in the client. |
| VOICE-ro-b-2 | **End of speech is on the box's clock.** The process names a sample; the client maps it to when it forwarded that audio, taking the line's audio as real time (a frame ending at sample n arrives when sample n was spoken), and never later than the transcript's arrival. It keeps the last 4096 frames' positions; an end older than those is extrapolated back from the oldest, which only moves the one-moment clip earlier. | VOICE-ro-a-C6, C7 | If the line buffers audio unevenly, mark frames with the modem's own timestamps instead. |
| VOICE-ro-b-3 | **Audio is bounded by its text, not checked by content.** A sentence's audio is at most 1 s plus 250 ms a rune, 40 s in all; longer fails the sentence. The bound limits how much a synthesizer can add, but it cannot tell what the audio says: a synthesizer that speaks other words than it was given is answered by what it is fed (only sentences the CH-21 check passed), its sandbox (no network, one call), and pinned public weights (b2), not by this client. | Brief rule 3 for b; rearchitecture note §2.3 | A transcribe-back check of each sentence (b2, on the M6 Ultra) if the pins cannot be trusted that far. |
| VOICE-ro-b-4 | **A sentence the wire cannot carry is refused, not altered.** `Speak` refuses text with a control or format character rather than replacing it, because the CH-21 check ran on the text as given. The loop cuts sentences at line breaks (VOICE-ro-a-C4), so only tabs and format characters reach this refusal, and the loop plays its failed clip. | VOICE-ro-a-C3 | If answers carry tabs in normal use, fold them in the loop before the check. |
| VOICE-ro-b-5 | **One session per call.** A Client serves one call: one `Listen`, whose context's end ends the session, and any number of `Speak` calls. The process is started per call in a sandbox (vm/gvisor `StartStream`), which is VOICE-ro-d's wiring; the client takes the stream's two ends. | Brief VOICE-ro-b row ("one sandboxed untrusted process") | — |
| VOICE-ro-b-6 | **No engine in the default build.** `agentos-speech` refuses to start without an engine; b2 adds the recognizer and synthesizer behind a build tag, with their weights pinned, and measures turn timing on the M6 Ultra. The fixed end-of-turn cap VOICE-ro-a-C6 leaves to b is measured there too, since it is a property of the recognizer's silence detection. | Hardware hold (dispatch 2026-11-09) | — |
