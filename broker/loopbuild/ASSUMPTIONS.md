# Loop 1 builder machines: assumptions

Built for BOARD W3-builder (W3 step 3c) against SPEC v0.12 LOOP-1, LOOP-2, LOOP-6, CHG-1, OP-8 and REV-5, under the lens rulings C-3c-1 to C-3c-6 (design /mnt/project-files/w3/step3-builders-design.md). Each row is a reading of the spec or of a ruling, or a gap left for a later package, that a reviewer may want to change.

| # | Assumption | Spec basis | If it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **One fresh private machine per job (C-3c-1).** `Build` creates `lb-<random>` from the builder image in the experiments admission class, labelled private, with `DefaultMemMB` (768 MB, the N95 lens note). It is never forked from another machine. It is checked on creation and at every poll: on the builder image, not forked, and labelled private. Otherwise the job ends with `ErrNotClean`. The machine is destroyed when the job ends, whatever the outcome, and `Cleanup` destroys any left from a previous run. One job runs at a time. | LOOP-1, REV-5, RES-1 | — |
| B2 | **The socket is the builder's whole reach (C-3c-2).** It serves `GET /brief`, `POST /candidate` and the metered model route under `/model/`, with at most 4 requests in flight. Everything else is 404, so there is no `/mcp` (executors, `managed_tree`) and no `/owner`. `Services` hands a builder machine its job's socket or nothing, never the guest plane. agentosd routes `lb-` machines only to the builder (`lateServices.build`). | ARC-6, REV-5 | — |
| B3 | **The brief.** The brief carries the hypothesis's signal, class and key, the namespace a candidate may write, and the limits. It also carries the journal intents behind the hypothesis, as the journal keeps them: free text redacted, broker intents left out, at most 256. Of the dev split it carries only explicit cases (change C17). A task the owner accepted only implicitly (verdict source ending `-implicit`) is counted, never read: its steps keep account, action and state but no params (security F1 on #126). Held-out cases never reach Loop 1's builders (CHG-1). | CHG-1, LOOP-4 | — |
| B4 | **The builder is treated as compromised (C-3c-4).** A submission must be 1 to 32 files. Each must be valid UTF-8 text with no NUL and at most 64 KiB, under the class's one namespace at a clean relative path, with at most 512 KiB in all. Anything else gets 422 and may be sent again within the job's time. The candidate the builder returns has the source set to local and Public left false. Loop 1 then checks `inClass`, stamps the source and origin, and marks the candidate never public, because the builder is `loops.Private` (loops L22). There is no builder for any class but procedures, skills and context, so none writes security, routing, update or image namespaces. The change pipeline's new-identifier check covers every text file those classes hold, context rules included (change C20). | LOOP-6, CHG-6, REV-5 | — |
| B5 | **Model use (C-3c-5, C-3c-6).** Calls are metered on the spare meter. The builder share (prefix `lb-`) may use at most 35% of it and reserves nothing, while evaluation keeps its reserve. One job may use at most `DefaultJobTokens` (200,000). The check runs against the meter's use for the job's machine, which is fresh per job, so that use is the job's. A call past the cap gets 429. One model call runs at a time per job, so concurrent calls cannot overshoot the cap (security R3 on #126). A job ends after `DefaultTimeout` (30 min). The route forwards with the machine's label, which is private. The vault process gives `lb-` machines the grants of `-builder-from`, always as private data, and never grants of their own (egress K15). Without `-builder-from`, builders have no model access. | OP-8, LOOP-2, REV-5, CAP-9 | Numbers are first settings; adjust with measured use. |
| B6 | **No builder without the box's image (C-3c-3).** Builder machines run a minimal builder image, a toolchain and the brief client, never the agent's: agentosd refuses a `-builder-image` equal to `-agent-image` by name or directory (security R2 on #126). That image is `guest/builder/build-rootfs.sh`: the brief client `agentos-builder` alone (BOARD W3-builder-image). Without `-builder-image`, agentosd attaches no builder. Loop 1's builder for signals other than repeats is then never `Ready`, so no job is offered and nothing is measured against the loop. | LOOP-1 | W3-builder-image builds it. |
| B7 | **Preemption (RES-1).** A builder machine that is not running is resumed when capacity allows, within the job's time. A job's ctx ending stops it. Either way the machine is destroyed. A candidate taken before ctx ended is still returned. | RES-1, LOOP-1 | — |
| B8 | **Builder IDs are reserved (security R1 on #126).** `vm.BuilderPrefix` (`lb-`) machines are made only by Create, for this builder. No fork or merge makes or touches one, so the vault's builder grants never reach an agent's fork. | REV-5, ARC-6 | — |
| B9 | **The socket is capped (L3 MUST 2 on #126).** A builder holds at most 4 connections to its socket. Further ones wait in the kernel's queue and cost agentosd no file descriptor. A request must be read within 30 s. The guest plane's limit listener is restated here because builder code never links the guest plane. The meter forgets a machine once its use has left the window, so one-job machines do not pile up (L3 S4). | RES-1, OP-8 | — |
| B10 | **Visible when not running (UX-126-1).** When `-builder-image` is set and the builder does not start, STATUS says "Learning from failed, corrected, slow or costly tasks: not running. Restarting the box may fix it." With no `-builder-image` it says "Learning: this box learns from repeated routines only, not yet from mistakes or slow tasks." (potency R3 on #126; worded per UX-134-1, which implies no setting the owner could look for). After the owner turns learning off, it says neither (UX R1 on #126). | LOOP-1 | — |
| B11 | **A builder may give up (W3-builder-image).** `POST /done` ends the job with no candidate, so the one builder slot is not held until the job's time runs out. It ends only its own job, which a builder could already do by stalling, so it adds no authority. The brief client sends it after its rounds or once the model route refuses. A job ends once: after `/done` or an accepted candidate, either gets 409. The round limit is the guest's own and binds nothing; the broker bounds a job only by its token cap and its time (B5). | LOOP-1, RES-1 | — |
| B12 | **Every job is counted (potency R1 on #126).** Each job logs one line: its machine, signal, outcome (candidate, no candidate, timed out, machine refused, stopped; "token cap" when it ended at JobTokens), tokens and time. No brief or candidate content. Retune JobTokens and Timeout from the first 20 jobs. | OP-8, LOOP-2 | Move the counts into the learning digest or STATUS if the log is not read. |
