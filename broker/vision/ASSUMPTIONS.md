# Camera vision service: assumptions

What `broker/vision`, `broker/vision/supervise`, `broker/vision/faces` and `broker/cmd/agentos-vision` rest on (briefs/VIS-core.md; part (a), VIS-svc; parts (c1) and (c2), VIS-faces). Each row is a reading of the brief or spec, or a property the code relies on, that a reviewer may want to change.

| # | Assumption | Spec basis | If it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| VIS-A1 | Part (a) links no runtime, as INFER-A1: `agentos-vision` built from it answers every call Unavailable, and the tests use a fake runtime. Part (e) adds `runtime_<name>.go`, the models and their pins; part (f) starts the service from agentosd. | brief split (a), (e), (f) | Nothing reaches the service before (f) wires it. |
| VIS-A2 | The service returns no track id, although the brief's design point 2 lists one. Tracking runs in the client (point 2, later; part b), and a service-chosen number per detection would be one more field a compromised service could fill with bits. | DEV-7; brief point 2 | If (b) needs the service's own association (for example appearance cues), it gets a closed signal (same or new, per previous box), never a free integer. |
| VIS-A3 | Confidence is an integer percent from 1 to 100. That is the brief's "quantised to two decimals" held exactly: a JSON float such as 0.87 cannot be checked for two decimals without rounding, and an integer can. | brief point 2 | None; a consumer that wants 0 to 1 divides by 100. |
| VIS-A4 | An error reply carries a cause from the four and no detail. INFER-SVC passes a cleaned detail through; here that would be 200 bytes of service-chosen text per call reaching a log, which is a channel for an encoding. The client's own `Error.Detail` is fixed text it writes. | DEV-7, A16 as amended by #3497 | A cause the owner needs to see is added to the closed list. |
| VIS-A5 | The supervisor sends the service's stdout and stderr to /dev/null (`QuietChild` in infer/supervise), and its own lines name the service (`Name`). Its STATUS cause stays the exit status and the oom mark, which the supervisor writes, not the service. A panic trace from the service is lost with the rest of its output. | DEV-7; INF-8 | Diagnostics the owner needs come back through the protocol's closed causes, never through the service's text. |
| VIS-A6 | vision/supervise reuses infer/supervise's jail, leaf and restart loop rather than copying them, adding only the two options above. The service runs as its own user (part f adds `agentos-vision`), never as agentos-infer's, because it will hold templates (part c). | RES-2; brief point 1 | A jail difference between the two services goes into infer/supervise as another option, so the two never drift. |
| VIS-A7 | A frame is decoded pixels (rgb24, gray8, or y16 for thermal) sent raw after a JSON header line, not base64 on the line: CAM-clerk delivers decoded frames, and base64 would cost a third more bytes and an encode per frame on the N95. The largest frame is 3840x2160 rgb24 (about 24 MiB); the service holds at most `-concurrent` payloads (2 by default): it reads a payload only once a slot is free, and a runtime call that outlives its deadline keeps its frame and slot until it returns (a timeout reloads the runtime, which must make the call return; if it has not returned three call timeouts later, the service exits and the supervisor restarts it), so neither a burst nor a hung runtime holds more than two frames. | RES-2; brief point 1 | Part (e) measures the floor; a smaller cap lowers `MaxWidth` and `MaxHeight`. |
| VIS-A8 | The class list (person, vehicle, dog, cat, bird, deer, fox, coyote, bear, unknown-animal) and the sound list (alarm, glass-break, gunshot, scream, dog-bark, vehicle, knock, doorbell) are fixed in code; changing either is a reviewed PR. There is no speech label. | DEV-7; brief point 2, Mark 2026-10-27 (sound events only) | Part (e) may narrow the lists to what its models can tell apart; widening them is a reviewed change. |
| VIS-A9 | Plate text (`[A-Z0-9 -]{1,10}`, no space at either end) is a residual channel: about 50 bits per vehicle detection. Raw detections reach only the tracker, the owner's page and the drone fixes; planners and the bus see rate-limited track outputs (part b, DEV-8). | DEV-8; brief point 2 | (b)'s events carry plate text only quoted (`QuotedPlate`), and only for a vehicle on a consistent track. |
| VIS-A10 | `Client.Enrolled` is `faces.Faces.Enrolled`: the ids the last lend that landed carried, from the client's own `clerk.Secrets` slots, never the service's word. Nil (part a, or no faces side) refuses every `enrolled:<id>` reply. | DEV-7 | None. |
| VIS-A11 | The leaf, OOM, jail and quiet-output tests skip off root or without a delegated cgroup; CI's machines step runs `./vision/...` with `./infer/...` as root with no_new_privs and fails on any skip (INFER-A8). | RES-2 | As INFER-A8. |
| VIS-A12 | The enrolment and lend calls are served on a second socket, the control socket (fd 4, `supervise.ControlPath`, beside the feed socket in the same 0700 directory), and the feed's calls on the first only. A feed that later reaches the first socket (part f) can neither lend a template nor ask for one. | DEV-7; comment 6461801310 point 4 (an option on infer/supervise, never a copy of the jail) | None. |
| VIS-A13 | The kernel's content socket for the `vision` store admits the uid of the side holding the template slots (`vision/faces`), never the service's uid, as comment 6459973175 point 1 proposed. A service that could call `Seal` could keep an unknown face's encoding as content, and one that could call `ReadFor` could log reads under any lineage. Part (f) runs the faces side under its own uid, or on an `asUID` thread as `owner/kernelsock` does, and adapts the content socket's client to `faces.Content`. | DEV-7, D-207, LOG-4 | If (f) puts the faces side in agentosd, `vision/faces` is added to agentosd's ARC-2 list then. |
| VIS-A14 | A template is little-endian float32s, at most 1024 (`MaxTemplate`), compared by cosine similarity in the service against `-match` (0.6 by default; part e sets it per encoder). The service keeps templates scaled to unit length, in memory only, and a lend replaces the whole set and zeroes the old one. Each encoding the runtime returns (`Seen.Face`, `Encode`) is zeroed before the call that made it returns, on every path, a timeout included. | DEV-7, D-207 | Part (e) may change the dimension or the measure. A wider template is a reviewed change to `MaxTemplate`. |
| VIS-A15 | Erase is by key: each template is sealed under its own random wrap key, and the wrap key is kernel content (class `vision-wrap-key`) under the person's subject. Every lend reads each wrap key again with `ReadFor` under the one lineage `svc-vision`. A lend runs at each enrolment change, on `Rewind` (agentosd's D-169 rewind of `svc-vision`, wired in part f), and every 15 minutes in `Run`. So after an erase, the service drops the template at once when the rewind runs, and within 15 minutes otherwise. The cost is one `content.read` entry per enrolled person per lend, about 96 a day each. | LOG-4, D-169; comment 6459973175 point 2 | A shorter bound costs more log entries. A kernel call that answers "erased?" without a read would remove the cost; that would be a kernel change. |
| VIS-A16 | A restarted service holds no templates until the next lend. Part (f) runs `Faces.Sync` whenever the supervisor reports the service up. Until then every face is `unknown`, which fails closed. Backups carry no content yet (#3559), so after a restore every wrap key is absent and every enrolled person shows as `unknown` until re-enrolled. `faces.ConsentNotice`, which the enrolment page shows and the anchor's tap binds (VIS-A17), says so. | DEV-7; comment 6459973175 point 3 | None. |
| VIS-A17 | Enrolment is the owner's tier-4 step (DEV-7, CH-3), and the page that runs it is on the untrusted side, so `vision/faces` checks the approval itself, as the kernel checks `BackupTarget`: `Begin` names one enrolment (a fresh nonce, the id, the subject, the frame's digest and `ConsentNotice`'s) as a change for an anchor's passkey to co-sign over `key.CosignMsg`, and `Enrol` makes a template only on that co-signature, by a key in `Anchors` (the kernel's `State().Anchors`), once and within 10 minutes; any refusal uses the change up, and at most 8 wait. A refused enrolment asks the service for nothing. Un-enrolment narrows, so it needs no tap. The page that shows the notice and asks for the tap is the owner side's (`broker/localui`, sub B's directory, docs/LANES.md), wired with part (f): it passes the WebAuthn assertion as agentosd's recovery flow does (`tapper`). | DEV-7, CH-3, KEY-4; comment 6459973175 point 1 (enrolment does not seal from `broker/localui`) | If the owner's phone passkey is to approve enrolment too, `Anchors` takes the live passkeys (`State().Passkeys()`) instead. |
| VIS-A18 | A planner's only way into VIS-core is a `vision.Ask` checked by `CheckAsk`: an op from `PlannerOps` (none in part c; part b adds its track-level outputs), a camera and zone from the enrolment-id alphabet, and a class from the closed list. An Ask has no field that can hold a crop, a frame, an encoding or free text, and an op with a word such as find, match, search, identify or follow is refused with `ErrNoMatch`; every other undeclared op with `ErrNotOffered`. The clearest-unknown-face evidence references (brief point 4) need a visit, which is a track, so they move to part (b). | DEV-7, D-207; brief points 4 and 8 | Part (b) adds each op to `PlannerOps` with a test that its answer type passes `carrier` (`TestNoCaptureLeavesVIS`). |
