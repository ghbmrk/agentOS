# graph: assumptions

Built for TN-graph (#2857, brief `briefs/TN-graph.md`) against KRN-11, CLK-10, CH-11, CH-12, CLK-11 and D-149, D-151, D-152. The package is advisory: nothing imports it yet, and wiring STATUS, `STOP <name>` and proposals to it is later rows' work. Each row is a reading a reviewer may want to change.

| # | Assumption | Basis | If it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TN-graph-A1 | The owner's reach starts at nodes of kind person with `Owner` set; a person node without `Owner` is someone else. | Brief, cut-off sets | Change `reach` in cut.go. |
| TN-graph-A2 | A brake bounds an asset when it is `Set` and a watch edge into the brake or the asset comes from a node the thief does not hold. A held brake node is not read as unset: the thief changing it is what the witness sees. Whether removing a held brake is itself delayed is #3196 (later). | KRN-11, brief "What a thief takes" | Add `!comp[b]` to `bounded` and to the test's `naiveTaken`. |
| TN-graph-A3 | `New` refuses more than the brief lists: a brake edge whose From is not a brake node, an empty or unknown source, a repeated From, and an unknown kind or asset class. A graph whose measurement cannot say where an edge came from would miscount confidence (CLK-10). | CLK-10, brief "Edges" | Relax the checks in `New`. |
| TN-graph-A4 | `Confidence(class)` counts every edge kind (cutoff, brake and watch included) whose To is an asset of the class or an ancestor of one over reset, copy and act edges: those are the edges its cut sets and cut-off sets rest on. | CLK-10, brief "Source and confidence" | Filter by edge kind in `Confidence`. |
| TN-graph-A5 | `Rotation(id)` lists accounts only (kind account) in the reset closure, which fires on any From like `Reach`; the copies are what `id` itself holds a copy of, not copies held further along. | CH-11, D-151 | Change the kind filter or close over copy edges in `Rotation`. |
| TN-graph-A6 | `Resolve` trims spaces and matches a Name or an ID, ignoring case, and succeeds only when exactly one node matches either way. A Name two nodes share, or an ID that is another node's Name, returns false, so the caller pauses everything: an over-wide pause costs only a pause, a wrong one does not (L3 and Security on #3193). | CH-11, D-151 | Return every match and let the caller pause their union. |
| TN-graph-A7 | When no class has an asset, `Weakest` returns false and `Line` reads "Nothing to protect is recorded yet." A class with no assets is never the weakest. `Score` carries an `Assets` count for this. With no next action, `Line` says "Nothing needed now." only when the weakest class needs more than two failures, and "No fix is known yet." otherwise. (Later note on #3184, UX on #3193.) | CH-12, brief "Scoreboard" | Change `Board.Weakest` and `Line`. |
| TN-graph-A8 | A STATUS line names a failure domain by the names of the nodes that carry it, never by its label, and words it as one failure: "lost to one failure shared by Key 1 and Key 2", "of Ana's phone", or "of its own maker or provider" when the domain is the asset's own, labelled or not, carried by no other node (UX on #3193). An asset with several classes gets one single-cut line per domain, under the first of money, identity, safety it carries. The fix is the highest-ranked candidate (Next's order) whose result no longer has that single cut and that weakens no asset (TN-graph-A16). | CH-12, CLK-11, A22 Graph | Change `Lines` in status.go. |
| TN-graph-A9 | Every STATUS line, not just the first, is cut to 160 characters and kept to GSM-7's basic set; a character outside printable ASCII, or one GSM-7 has only in its extension table (`` ` [ ] { } \ ^ ~ | ``), becomes `?`, so a character is one septet. | CH-12 | Change `gsm`. |
| TN-graph-A10 | Downtime treats a window edge as a log entry only on a side with no entry at or beyond it, so a quiet stretch at either end of the window counts when it is longer than the gap. With no entries at all, the whole window is downtime, whatever the gap. | D-152, brief "Downtime" | Change `Downtime`'s boundary handling. |
| TN-graph-A11 | `Count` counts kinds account and device; keys, credentials and the box's nodes are not devices. `CountLine`'s change is the change in accounts plus devices; zero reads "no change this month". | D-152 | Change `Count` or `CountLine`. |
| TN-graph-A12 | `Idle` lists accounts whose last use is strictly older than the threshold; `DefaultIdle` is 365 days. | D-152 | Change `Idle` or `DefaultIdle`. |
| TN-graph-A13 | `Next` and `Lines` drop a candidate that does not apply (an unknown brake, a dropped edge not in the graph, a malformed edge), rather than failing the whole list. | CH-12 | Return the error to the caller. |
| TN-graph-A14 | Cut sets enumerate every single domain and pair, each a fixed point over the edges: quadratic in domains, fine for a household's tens of domains. | KRN-11 | Index edges by From if graphs grow to hundreds of domains. |
| TN-graph-A15 | Missing data is weak: `New` gives a node with no failure domain, other than a person or brake node, a domain of its own (`SelfDomain`, `self:<ID>`), so an unlabelled account or device can still fall alone, through its own maker or provider. Owners are not modelled as a failure domain. (Security on #3193.) | KRN-11, CLK-10 | Drop the default in `New`, or make declarations (CLK-14) require a label. |
| TN-graph-A16 | A proposal *weakens* when any asset, in any class, is worse off after: the owner reached it and no longer does (a lock-out); a provider can now cut the owner off from it; a single domain now takes it that did not before; or a pair now takes it when nothing under three failures did before. A new pair beside an existing single or pair is no regression, since the smallest cut does not shrink, so swapping a one-device sign-in for two independent keys is offered. A swapped single (phone for tablet) still weakens. `Next` and the STATUS fixes never offer a proposal that weakens, whatever its summed gain (KRN-11, D-152; Security, L3 and Potency on #3193). `Effect.Steps` (the fall in single cuts across every asset and class, plus the fall in cut-off assets, leaving out an asset the owner is locked out of) counts progress that the per-class minimum misses. `Next` takes a candidate with gain or steps above zero and ranks it structural first, then by gain per minute, then by steps per minute. `Effect.Gain` stays the plain board difference shown before and after: a lock-out can lower `CutOff` there, but it always weakens, so it is never offered. | KRN-11, CH-12 | Change `Effect`, `weakens`, `steps`, `rank` and `Next` in board.go. |
