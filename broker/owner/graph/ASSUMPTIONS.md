# graph: assumptions

Built for TN-graph (#2857, brief `briefs/TN-graph.md`) against KRN-11, CLK-10, CH-11, CH-12, CLK-11 and D-149, D-151, D-152. The package is advisory: nothing imports it yet, and wiring STATUS, `STOP <name>` and proposals to it is later rows' work. Each row is a reading a reviewer may want to change.

| # | Assumption | Basis | If it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TN-graph-A1 | The owner's reach starts at nodes of kind person with `Owner` set; a person node without `Owner` is someone else. | Brief, cut-off sets | Change `reach` in cut.go. |
| TN-graph-A2 | A brake bounds an asset when it is `Set` and a watch edge into the brake or the asset comes from a node the thief does not hold. A held brake node is not read as unset: the thief changing it is what the witness sees. Whether removing a held brake is itself delayed is #3196 (later). | KRN-11, brief "What a thief takes" | Add `!comp[b]` to `bounded` and to the test's `naiveTaken`. |
| TN-graph-A3 | `New` refuses more than the brief lists: a brake edge whose From is not a brake node, an empty or unknown source, a repeated From, an unknown kind or asset class, and the brake, owner and non-person-owner edges and nodes of A15. | TN-graph-A4 | `Confidence(class)` counts every edge kind (cutoff, brake and watch included) whose To is an asset of the class or an ancestor of one over reset, copy and act edges: those are the edges its cut sets and cut-off sets rest on. | CLK-10, brief "Source and confidence" | Filter by edge kind in `Confidence`. |
| TN-graph-A5 | `Rotation(id)` lists accounts only (kind account) in the reset closure, which fires on any From like `Reach`; the copies are what `id` itself holds a copy of, not copies held further along. | CH-11, D-151 | Change the kind filter or close over copy edges in `Rotation`. |
| TN-graph-A6 | `Resolve` trims spaces and matches a Name or an ID, ignoring case, and succeeds only when exactly one node matches either way. A Name two nodes share, or an ID that is another node's Name, returns false, so the caller pauses everything: an over-wide pause costs only a pause, a wrong one does not (L3 and Security on #3193). | CH-11, D-151 | Return every match and let the caller pause their union. |
| TN-graph-A7 | When no class has an asset, `Weakest` returns false and `Line` reads "Nothing to protect is recorded yet." (Later note on #3184, UX on #3193.) | CH-12, brief "Scoreboard" | Change `Board.Weakest` and `Line`. |
| TN-graph-A8 | A STATUS line names a failure domain by the names of the nodes that carry it, never by its label, and words it as one failure: "lost to one failure shared by Key 1 and Key 2", "of Ana's phone", or "of its own maker or provider" when the domain is the asset's own, labelled or not, carried by no other node (UX on #3193). The fix is the highest-ranked candidate (Next's order) whose result no longer has that single cut and that weakens no asset (TN-graph-A16). | CH-12, CLK-11, A22 Graph | Change `Lines` in status.go. |

| TN-graph-A10 | Downtime treats a window edge as a log entry only on a side with no entry at or beyond it, so a quiet stretch at either end of the window counts when it is longer than the gap. With no entries at all, the whole window is downtime, whatever the gap. | D-152, brief "Downtime" | Change `Downtime`'s boundary handling. |
| TN-graph-A11 | `Count` counts kinds account and device; keys, credentials and the box's nodes are not devices. `CountLine`'s change is the change in accounts plus devices; zero reads "no change this month". | D-152 | Change `Count` or `CountLine`. |

| TN-graph-A13 | `Next` and `Lines` drop a candidate that does not apply (an unknown brake, a dropped edge not in the graph, a malformed edge), rather than failing the whole list. | CH-12 | Return the error to the caller. |
| TN-graph-A14 | Cut sets enumerate every single domain and pair, each a fixed point over the edges: quadratic in domains, fine for a household's tens of domains. | KRN-11 | Index edges by From if graphs grow to hundreds of domains. |
| TN-graph-A15 | Missing data is weak, and every node but the owner can fail. `New` gives any node with no failure domain a domain of its own (`SelfDomain`, `self:<ID>`), a person (such as a recovery contact) or a brake included, except the owner's own person node, the one node not modelled as failing. So that no node without a failure domain can stand in for a credential or a witness, `New` refuses a reset, copy, act, cutoff or watch edge from a brake (a brake is a setting that only brakes), an AND edge or a watch edge that includes the owner (what proves the owner, or tells them, can fail), and an owner node that is not a person. The owner acting alone is still a control source: that is how reach is measured (A1). (Security on #3193, rework of the reject at `5a0b304`.) | KRN-11, CLK-10 | Drop the default or a refusal in `New`, and `naiveDomains` and the generators in the test. |
| TN-graph-A16 | **Non-regression invariant.** A proposal is offered (by `Next`, or as a STATUS `Fix:`) only if (1) it adds no owner node, so reach is always measured from the same owner; (2) for every asset and class in the after state, new ones included, measured against its before state: the owner still reaches it if they did before, it gains no cut-off provider, and every thief cut it gains (a single domain or a pair) is larger than its old smallest cut; and (3) no class on the board scores worse (ThiefNeed does not fall, CutOff does not rise), so the gain is at least zero. A new asset's before state is its floor: one failure of a domain only it carries (its own maker or provider), no other cut, smallest cut taken as 1, no cut-off provider. (2) is `Effect.Weakens`, (3) is `Effect.Lowers`, (1) is refused by `Apply`. Under (2) a second pair beside a pair, or a swapped single, is refused; a pair beside a single is not, since the smallest cut does not shrink. `TestWeakensInvariantOverFixtureMutations` restates (2) from brute-force cuts and checks it over every one-step mutation of Ana: each edge dropped; single, AND and cut-off edges added onto each asset; new money accounts under each kind of label; and, for every node kind (its list is asserted equal to the engine's, so a new kind fails until covered), a new node labelled or not as a control source onto each asset, as one member of an AND on each control edge, as a witness and as a cut-off provider. The restatement applies its own default-domain rule and also checks each mutation's cut sets against brute force. A brake in any of those roles, the owner in an AND or as a witness, and an added owner of any kind are refused by `Apply`. The invariant rests on every node a thief could use carrying a failure domain, which A15 enforces. Among proposals that pass, `Next` takes one with gain or `Steps` above zero (`Steps` is the fall in single cuts across every asset and class plus the fall in cut-off assets, leaving out any lock-out). It ranks them structural first, then by gain per minute, then by steps per minute. (Security, L3 and Potency on #3193.) | KRN-11, D-152, CH-12 | Change `Apply`, `Effect`, `weakens`, `Lowers`, `rank` and `Next` in board.go, and the restatement in the test. |
